# Backlog: DTM 2010 pack

Everything still open for the 2010 pack, as of 13 November 2026. Era-wide
items (lap-time calibration, two stops, engine sounds, tracks to trace,
logos) are in the [2003 backlog](DTM2003_BACKLOG.md), since 2010 builds on
`bases/dtm-era3` with 2003 as the mother template. Game limits are in
[DEVELOPER_WISHLIST.md](../DEVELOPER_WISHLIST.md).

Priority: **P1** = changes how the game plays, **P2** = more of the real
season, **P3** = presentation.

- **Tracks** · P2: Adria is traced (2003's spec, 2010 laps and wear in
  `gen.json`). The 3.736 km Hockenheim and the Shanghai street circuit are
  still placeholders: the Hockenheim layout needs a source, and Shanghai
  is not in OSM (see the 2003 backlog).
- **Laps** · P2: Norisring, Nürburgring, Zandvoort and Oschersleben lap
  counts differ between sources; Adria 38 or 59 laps; Shanghai 49 or 50 of
  60 (`research/season.md`).
- **Older-car weight** · P2: ru.wiki says 2008 cars ran lighter; unverified,
  not used.
- **Sound recordings** · P2: 2010 A4 and W204 C-Klasse recordings would
  need the overlay's `recording_years` widened beyond 2003 (see the 2003
  backlog).
- **O'Young's nationality** · P3: Chinese in the pack, Hong Kong per en.wiki.
- **Sponsors, colours, car images, logos, portraits** · P3: as for 2003.
