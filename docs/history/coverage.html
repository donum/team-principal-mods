Best order from here

Each step adds the lever that unlocks the most seasons, given the ones already on. The bar is the share of all seasons playable after that step.

Needs per season: DTM from the rulebook research of every season (docs/dtm_rules.html), the other series from the rules history tables in docs/history. Only rules that shape every race weekend count; rare cases like half points or red-flag aggregates are left out. Game support as of Team Principal 0.99.5 and the 0.99.6 beta. "Partly" levers have a stand-in today that behaves differently, so they still count as missing. Lever IDs are items in docs/DEVELOPER_WISHLIST.md; IDs starting with N are not in the wishlist yet.
