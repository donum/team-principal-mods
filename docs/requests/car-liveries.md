# More car liveries, more often

- **Attach:** mock-ups A, B, C, D and H (made in Claude Design), copies in `img/car-liveries/`

## Feature request: Improve immersion by showing more car liveries, more often

It's great how much a modder can customize the base game. I realized that a big part of immersion comes from the cars, and their liveries, in a series.

Today a mod can include one car image per team. In real series, team-mates often race different liveries; in the 2013 DTM almost every team ran two different designs. So my request is a car image per entry (car number). If an entry has no image of its own, the team image is used as today, so existing mods keep working.

Here is where it could appear (mock-ups attached):

**MOCK UP A: Race screen, driver panel**
Showing the portrait and car of the selected driver, and optionally a small car in the timing tower, makes it easy to recognise each car at a glance.

**MOCK UP B: Car Setup**
Today this screen shows a formula car that we cannot change. It would be great to show the entry's own livery. Since our cars face the other way, the front and rear controls would swap sides to match the image.

**MOCK UP C: Select Your Team**
Each driver in the list could show their portrait and car, and the big car image could follow the selected driver.

**MOCK UP D: Race results**
Today the results screen has no car or driver images. Cars and portraits on the podium, and small cars in the table, would make it more personal.

The upcoming endurance update might need more changes. MOCK UP H is a first idea for four drivers per car.

I'm curious what you think.

Dan
