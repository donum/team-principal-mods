## Idea: title sponsor liveries

As a follow-up to Seasonal Livery Support: it would be great if the livery could also change when a team's title sponsor changes.

In a career, title sponsors come and go, so a livery per season doesn't always match the game. A mod could add a car image per title sponsor, for example team_sponsor.png, shown while the team has that sponsor. Without one, the season or team image is used as today.

A lot of work for modders, but it would make later seasons feel much more real.
