# Ten more ideas for immersion

- **Status:** ideas, not posted
- **Mock-ups:** `img/immersion-ideas/` (one screen each, DTM 2013 at the
  Nürburgring, same race as `weekend-images.md`)

| # | Idea | What the player sees | Data the game already has |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Monday paper | A front page after each race weekend: winner, one photo, standings | Results, standings, next round |
| 2 | Driver voices | Two or three short lines from drivers after a session | Result, retirement cause, mood |
| 3 | Team radio | A radio log on the race screen | Pit stops, gaps, tyre wear |
| 4 | Season map | The calendar as a route across Europe, done rounds ticked | Calendar, track coordinates |
| 5 | Title fight | Points after every round for the top five, portraits at the line ends | Standings history |
| 6 | Livery reveal | The car before and after a new sponsor | Sponsor deals; images from the pack |
| 7 | TV battle | A broadcast graphic when two cars are close | Gaps in the race |
| 8 | Home race | The drivers racing in front of their home crowd | Driver and track nationality |
| 9 | Season album | One photo per round with the team result | Results; photos from the pack |
| 10 | Contract signed | A press release card when a driver signs | Contracts |

Driver lines (2, 3) and the paper (1) are templates filled from results,
written in the in-game style: short sentences, no judgements. The values in
5 and 9 are samples.
