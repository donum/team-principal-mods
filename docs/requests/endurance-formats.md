## Feature request: endurance weekend formats for every mode

Hey! I'm really looking forward to the endurance update.

Endurance will need things the game doesn't have yet: races in hours, more than one qualifying session, qualifying by class, a Hyperpole, warm-up and night practice, driver crews.

My request: could these be built as general weekend settings that every mode can use, not only endurance? A weekend would be a list of sessions, and each race picks where its grid comes from.

That would open up many series at once:
- **F1:** two timed sessions (older seasons), the two hour race limit, sprint weekends (the sprint result sets the GP grid).
- **DTM:** two races per weekend with one qualifying each (2015 on), and heats where race 2 starts in the order of race 1 (1988 to 1995).
- **BTCC:** three races per meeting, race 2 from race 1, race 3 with the top 6 to 12 reversed by a draw.
- **NLS:** 4 and 6 hour races, 100+ cars, three start groups a few minutes apart, one to four drivers per car.

Grid options I would need: qualifying, previous race, reversed top N, reversed by draw.

The oval mode already has a rolling start and stage points. Formula packs would love those too.

I'm curious what you think.

Dan
