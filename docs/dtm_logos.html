DTM logos by era

From 2000 to October 2024 the DTM kept one wordmark: heavy italic capitals. Only the colours and finish changed. Pick the logo by season year, not by technical era: eras 4 and 6 each span two looks.

  1. 1984 – 1996 DPM · DTM · ITC Eras 1 and 2

    Not verified yet. No period source found for the DPM, the old DTM or the ITC logo. Use a plain “DTM” text title for these packs until a programme, poster or TV still turns up. Don’t reuse a Masters-era logo here.

    Open question

  2. 2000 – 2004 Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters Era 3

    Italic “DTM” in chrome / silver gradient on navy blue, with “DEUTSCHE TOURENWAGEN MASTERS” stacked beside it.

    dtm.de, April 2002 (Wayback)

  3. 2005 – 2014 DTM as a brand Era 3, era 4 to 2014

    Same chrome-on-navy italic letters, now without the long name. From 2005 “DTM” is a brand, not an abbreviation. Seen on dtm.com in 2008, 2013 and 2014.

    de.wikipedia: Dtm logo.png (2006) · dtm.com, June 2013 (Wayback)

  4. 2015 – 2018 Flat wordmark Era 4

    The gradient goes. Solid italic “DTM”, dark on white or white on navy. dtm.com switches in June 2015.

    Commons: DTM Blue and White logo.svg · dtm.com, June 2015 (Wayback)

  5. 2019 – 2022 Neon on navy Era 5, era 6 to 2022

    Same letters in neon yellow-green on navy blue.

    Commons: DTM logo.svg · dtm.com, June 2019 (Wayback)

  6. 2023 – Oct 2024 ADAC colours Era 6

    The ADAC buys the brand and keeps the letters, now yellow on black. The 2024 season adds a “DTM 40” anniversary badge.

    Commons: DTM logo 2023.svg · Commons: DTM 40 Years.jpg

  7. 2025 season on New design Era 6

    First new design since 2000, by UnitedSenses (Munich). New italic capitals with notches cut into all three letters, yellow on a black rounded box. First shown at the Hockenheim finale in October 2024.

    Commons: Logo 2025.png · Design Tagebuch, 28 Oct 2024

Years come from archived dtm.de and dtm.com pages (Wayback Machine) and from the Wikimedia Commons files linked in each row. The site redesigns are the best dated evidence, so a switch may have reached TV graphics a few months earlier or later.
