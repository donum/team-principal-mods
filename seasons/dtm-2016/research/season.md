# 2016 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 9 weekends with 2 races each, so 18 races. The season ran from 7 May to 16 October, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- 24 cars, 8 per make: Audi RS 5 DTM, BMW M4 DTM and Mercedes-AMG C63 DTM.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **Marco Wittmann** (BMW Team RMG) with 206 points, 4 ahead of Edoardo Mortara on 202. It was his second title.
  - Teams: Audi Sport Team Abt Sportsline
  - Manufacturers: Audi

## Regulations
- **Format:** the same as 2015.
  - Saturday: 40 min with no tyre change.
  - Sunday: 60 min with 1 mandatory pit stop and tyre change. Sources show "+1 lap".
  - Separate qualifying before each race.
  - Points: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for both races. de.wiki confirms this. The en.wiki table showing 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 is wrong, since the point totals only add up with the 25-point scale.
- **Performance weights** were reworked:
  - They were now set **after qualifying**, not before the weekend.
  - They were based on each make's fastest driver's theoretical best lap, built from the best sector times.
  - If another make was more than 0.1% slower, the faster make added 2.5 kg and the slower make removed 2.5 kg. Above a 0.2% gap, the step was 5 kg.
  - Base weight was 1,120 kg. BMW started 7.5 kg lighter because of its 2015 performance.
  - Regulatory limits: +20 kg and −15 / −22.5 kg. No car reached them.
  - Season averages: BMW −3.8 kg, Mercedes +1.8 kg, Audi +0.7 kg (de.wiki).
- **BMW aero concession:** its rear wing was allowed to be 50 mm wider.
- **DRS:**
  - Allowed from lap 2 when within 1.0 s at the line.
  - Each car had a set number of uses per race, depending on track length. Each activation cost 3 uses, and it could then be opened twice more in the same lap at no further cost.
- **Tyres:** Hankook, single supplier.
- **Engines:** the same allocation rules as 2015. Not re-documented for 2016.

## Cars and engines
- All three cars used naturally aspirated 4.0 L 90° V8 engines, about 460 hp:
  - Audi: Audi V8
  - BMW: P66
  - Mercedes: Mercedes-AMG/HWA
- Base weight 1,120 kg. Hewland gearbox.

## Teams and people
- Teams:
  - Mercedes: HWA I, HWA II, Mücke and ART, all under the "Mercedes-Benz DTM Team …" name.
  - Audi: Abt Sportsline, Abt, Phoenix and Rosberg.
  - BMW: RMG, RBM, MTEK and Schnitzer.
- Bases are the same as 2015. See entries.csv.
- Audi head of motorsport: Wolfgang Ullrich, in his last season. Dieter Gass took over on 1 January 2017.

## Storylines
- The champion, Wehrlein, left for F1. GP3 champion Esteban Ocon came in at ART.
  - Ocon left after round 5 for the Manor F1 seat.
  - 2015 European F3 champion Felix Rosenqvist replaced him.
- Adrien Tambay was injured in Zandvoort race 1. René Rast stood in for race 2.
- Rast also replaced Mattias Ekström at the Hockenheim final. For that round, Rast and Mike Rockenfeller swapped teams.
- Mortara won 5 races but lost the title by 4 points.
- The Hungaroring returned to the calendar.
- Over the season, BMW was the lightest car on average.
