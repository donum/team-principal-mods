# 2022 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 36th DTM season and the second under GT3 rules.
- 8 weekends × 2 races = 16 races, from 30 April (Portimão) to 9 October (Hockenheim).
- This was ITR's last season as organiser; ITR was wound up at the end of 2022.
- **Champion:** Sheldon van der Linde (Schubert Motorsport, BMW M4 GT3) with 164 points. Lucas Auer was second on 153 and René Rast third on 149.
- **Teams' title:** Schubert Motorsport.
- **Manufacturers' title:** Audi.
- **TV:** coverage moved from Sat.1 to ProSieben (ran).

## Regulations (changes vs 2021)
- **Cars and tyres:** GT3 with the DTM's own BoP (AVL). Michelin tyres.
- **BoP:** can now be changed at any time, including during a weekend and up to the last race.
- **Success ballast:** reduced to +25 kg for the winner, +15 kg for second and +5 kg for third (previously 25/18/15 kg).
- **Fastest lap:** 1 extra point, but only if the driver finishes in the top 10.
- **Qualifying:** 20 minutes per race, with 3-2-1 points.
- **Races:** 55 minutes plus 1 lap.
- **Mandatory pit stop:** must be taken between minute 10 and minute 40 (de.wiki: from the 11th minute to the end of the 40th minute).
  - It can now also be made under the safety car. The 2013 rule that excluded safety-car stops was dropped.
  - Rear wheels must be changed first.
  - At most 4 mechanics change wheels, plus 1 on the air lance and 1 on the lollipop. Previously it was 6 plus a lollipop man.
  - Only one car per lap may use a team's pit box.
  - Pit-lane speed limit: 60 km/h. Different pit-lane limits per car were reserved as a balancing tool.
- **Tyres:**
  - Saturday and Sunday: 3 sets of Michelin tyres per car.
  - Friday: 1 fresh set per driver, which must be handed back.
  - Each driver must spend at least 50% of free practice on track.
- **Full Course Yellow:** introduced (60 km/h, no overtaking). A pit stop under FCY does not count as the mandatory stop (de.wiki).
- **Starts:** the race director releases the start with the light. The pole-sitter may choose to start on the left or right side.
- **Team orders:** banned, with possible exclusion from the championship, as a reaction to the 2021 finale.
- **Points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1, plus 1 for fastest lap, plus 3-2-1 for qualifying.
- **Push-to-pass / DRS:** none documented.

## Teams and people
- **Left the DTM:**
  - ROWE (moved to endurance racing)
  - JP Motorsport
  - GetSpeed
- **New or expanded teams:**
  - Schubert: 2 × BMW M4.
  - Walkenhorst: expanded to 2 × M4.
  - GRT Grasser: 4 × Huracán, Lamborghini factory-backed.
  - Attempto: Audi.
  - KÜS Team Bernhard: team of Timo Bernhard, Porsche.
  - SSR Performance: 2 × Porsche.
- **T3 Motorsport:** contested only rounds 1–2.
- **Driver moves:**
  - Rast returned with Abt, replacing the retired Rockenfeller.
  - Feller (2021 ADAC GT Masters champion) joined Abt.
  - Götz moved to Winward. Stolz and Maini moved to HRT.
  - Engel returned with GruppeM after leaving in 2017.
  - Lawson left for F2 and Albon for F1 (Williams). Fraga and Cassidy took over at AF Corse.
  - Sébastien Loeb drove AF Corse #37 at Portimão.
- **Team principals:** not documented in the sources used. From general knowledge (unverified): Schubert – Torsten Schubert; GRT – Gottfried Grasser; KÜS Team Bernhard – Timo Bernhard (owner).

## Storylines
- Porsche's first DTM win: Preining at the Norisring R1, after a "chaos race".
- First DTM win for the BMW M4 GT3 (S. van der Linde, Lausitzring R1).
- Ferrari's last season with AF Corse. The team left after 2022.
- New circuits: Portimão and Imola. Spa returned.
- Dropped circuits: Zolder, Assen and Monza.
- The Norisring moved back to early July, and Hockenheim became the finale again.
- Auer said success ballast cost him the title (Autosport, after the 2022 season).
