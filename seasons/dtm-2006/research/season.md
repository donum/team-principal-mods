# 2006 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 10 rounds, one race each, from Hockenheim (9 Apr) to Hockenheim (29 Oct). The season was launched at the Düsseldorf presentation on 26 Mar (non-race).
- New venues: Brands Hatch (Indy circuit), Barcelona (National circuit) and Le Mans (Bugatti).
- Dropped: Spa, Istanbul, Brno.
- First season with only two makes after Opel's exit.
- **Champion:** Bernd Schneider (HWA AMG-Mercedes C-Klasse 2006), 71 points and 2 wins. It was his 5th DTM title (1995, 2000, 2001, 2003, 2006).
  - Runner-up: Bruno Spengler, 63 points and 4 wins.
  - 3rd: Tom Kristensen, 56.
  - Schneider clinched the title at Le Mans (round 9).
- **Teams' title:** Vodafone/Salzgitter AMG-Mercedes (Schneider/Green), 102 points.
- **Brand title:** Mercedes-Benz (de.wiki). Points were not found.
- Field: 20 cars, 10 Audi and 10 Mercedes. Each make ran 4 new cars, 4 one-year-old cars and 2 two-year-old cars (de.wiki DTM).

## Regulations
- **Cars:** same concept as 2005: CFRP monocoque, 4.0 L NA V8 with air restrictor, RWD, 6-speed sequential, control parts.
- **Development freeze:** "all major components including aerodynamics" of the 2005 cars had to be kept for 2006, including structural integrity and aero (Autosport, 9 Aug 2005). Audi's R12 plus and the 2006 C-Klasse were therefore evolutions.
- **Weights (Autosport, Aug 2005):**
  - New 2006 cars: 1,050 kg.
  - 2005-spec cars: 1,035 kg.
  - The 2004-spec cars' weight was not found.
- **Success ballast:**
  - The top manufacturer's latest-spec cars add 10 kg for the next round, and the worst manufacturer can remove 10 kg.
  - Maximum ±20 kg.
  - The handicap applied only to current cars; it was extended to the older generations in 2007 (de.wiki DTM).
- **Tyres:** Dunlop control tyre. The allocation was cut from 9 to **7 sets** per weekend (36 → 28 tyres). The pit crew size per stop was also reduced (Autosport).
- **Race:**
  - About 160–170 km, roughly 1 hour.
  - **Two compulsory pit stops** with tyre changes (en.wiki).
  - Refuelling with cans was allowed (70 L tank; de.wiki DTM and car articles).
- **Qualifying:** new F1-style knockout in three parts. The slowest six drop out after each part, and only each driver's fastest lap in a part counts (de.wiki DTM).
- **Points:** 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 to the top 8.

## Teams and people
- **Joest** left to focus on the Audi R10 TDI Le Mans programme.
- **Phoenix** (ex-Opel) and **Team Rosberg** (ex-Mercedes, base Neustadt/Weinstraße) returned with Audi year-old cars.
- **Futurecom TME:** Colin Kolles' team, based in Greding, running two-year-old Audis.
- **Persson** (Ingmar Persson) and **Mücke** expanded to three cars each.
- **Audi motorsport head:** Wolfgang Ullrich.

## Storylines
- Schneider started the season with two wins and took a record 5th title. Spengler won 4 of the last 5 races and finished runner-up.
- Martin Tomczyk took his first win (Barcelona) in his 6th DTM season.
- **Frentzen:** after being spun by an Audi teammate near season end, he criticised Audi on TV. His contract was not renewed (de.wiki DTM).
- **First women since the DTM restart:** Susie Stoddart (Mercedes) and Vanina Ickx (Audi).
- **Futurecom #19 seat carousel:** Tielemans, then Bleekemolen (sponsorship issues), then Kiesa (injured in a motocross accident), then Björk.
- Reigning champion Paffett left for the McLaren F1 test role. Alesi was demoted to a year-old Persson car in his final DTM season.
- **Brands Hatch Indy** (about 1.93 km) was the shortest track on the DTM calendar to date, shorter than the Norisring.
