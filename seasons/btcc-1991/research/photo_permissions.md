# BTCC 1991: photo permissions

Status: **drafts, nothing sent yet** (2026-11-16).

The pack has no period 1991 photos it may use yet. The four free menu
backgrounds (`title_bg_1` to `title_bg_4`, Wikimedia Commons) all show the
cars at revival meetings, not in 1991. For the cover and the main menu we
want real 1991 race photos.

The crops and the photo files are kept outside the repo:
`Pictures/mods/btcc-1991/backgrounds-crop/` (crops, `manifest.json` with the
source of each) and `Pictures/mods/btcc-1991/teams/*/ask_permission/`
(saved previews, `CREDITS_ask.csv`). Every candidate is listed in
`teams/_series/ask_permission/candidates.md`.

## Request 1: Motorsport Images (LAT and Sutton archive)

Getty Images bought Motorsport Images (with LAT and the Sutton archive) in
April 2024, and motorsportimages.com now sends you to Getty. No public
licensing email was found. Contact routes, best first:

1. LAT Images, the working arm that came with the deal: message form on
   https://www.latimages.com/, phone +44 (0)20 3405 8100 (London). Ask for
   the archive or licensing team.
2. Getty Images custom licensing: "Contact us" on gettyimages.com, or the
   UK sales office. Name the four Getty IDs.

Getty's website only sells editorial licences for these photos. A game mod
needs a custom licence, so it has to go through a person.

Expect a fee. Ask for a price for this small, free use before saying yes.

> Subject: Licence request: four 1991 BTCC photos for a free fan mod
>
> Hello,
>
> I make free fan mods for Team Principal, a motorsport manager game on
> Steam. I am building a pack for the 1991 British Touring Car Championship.
>
> I would like to use four 1991 photos from your archive:
>
> - LAT, Brands Hatch, 30 June 1991, Sugden and Hoy lead the field. Getty 2201200868
> - LAT, Donington Park, 15 September 1991, Cleland and Hoy lead the field. Getty 2263692772
> - LAT, Silverstone, 1 April 1991, the start. Getty 2201201086
> - Sutton, Silverstone 1991, Rouse leads into Copse. Alamy 3ABP9N9
>
> Use: one as the pack's cover picture on the Steam Workshop page, and all
> four as main menu backgrounds in the game. Cropped to 16:9, shown at
> 1920x1080 at most. No other edits.
>
> The mod is free. I do not sell it and there is no advertising. It is
> shared on the Steam Workshop and on my GitHub page. Each photo is credited
> as "LAT Images / Motorsport Images" or "Sutton Images / Motorsport Images"
> in the game credits and on the Workshop page, with a link if you want.
> The photos stay your copyright and are not part of the mod's own
> Creative Commons licence.
>
> Is this possible, and what would it cost? A reduced file of about 2500 px
> wide is enough.
>
> Thank you,
> Daniel Raude
> Team Principal Mods

When a yes comes in:

1. Save the written permission (mail or screenshot) next to the photo in
   `ask_permission/`, and move the files to a `permission/` folder.
2. Ask for the bigger file and redo the crop (`backgrounds-crop/make_crops.py`).
3. Add the credit to `credits.txt` the same way as Dennis de Wolde in the
   DTM 2013 pack: name, "used with permission", "keeps its copyright and is
   not covered by the pack's licence".
4. Rerun `tools/mod_browser.py`.

## If the answer is no

- Cover: build it from our own SVG car art (`art/svg/`), which needs no
  permission. A start scene with three or four of our cars works well.
- Menu: keep the four Commons revival photos, and ask the Flickr
  photographers in `candidates.md` (Anthony Fosh has period 1991
  Silverstone shots; his page asks for written permission).
