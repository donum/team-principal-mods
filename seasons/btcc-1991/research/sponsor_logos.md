# BTCC 1991: sponsor logos on the car drawings

Checked 2026-11-16. Every car drawing (`art/svg/liveries/*.json`) was compared again with the TV frames,
the TV graphics and the photos (left side, nose left). Sponsors with a known 1991 logo now use the real
logo (`"logos"` in the livery, rendered by `tools/livery_svg.py`). The logo files and their sources are kept
outside the repo in `Pictures/mods/btcc-1991/logos/` (`sources.csv` lists period, page and doubts per file).
Each livery's `note` ends with a "Logos (2026-11-16)" sentence on what is a real logo and what is not.

Esso, Shell (pecten and 1971 wordmark), Mobil 1, Mobil Pegasus, Yokohama (with the red Y), Pirelli, Hoosier,
Firestone, Champion, Koni, Castrol (script and roundel), Vauxhall griffin and wordmark, BMW roundel, Ford,
