# BTCC 1991 team facts

## Vic Lee Motorsport (Team Securicor + Listerine, Team Labatt's)
- Formed 1990 as a one-car team; earlier 1989 partnership of Vic Lee and Richard Asquith. 12 staff incl. secretarial (July 1991). https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/july-1991/78/british-grand-prix-preview-btcc-vic-lee-motorsport/
- Four M3s for two sponsors; Yokohama tyres; engines by Eurotech, Coventry; privateer BMW customer, own technical development. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/november-1991/40/british-touring-car-championship-2/
- Labatt's cars belonged to Laurence Bristow's Bristow Motorsport, run by VLM, Tim Harvey team-mate. BMW Motorsport Germany gave engineering support. (July 1991 article above)
- Base: Springhead Enterprise Park, Northfleet, Kent; later space at Peugeot Stoke Heath, Coventry. Won 1991 (Hoy) and 1992 (Harvey); Lee arrested 8 Sept 1992, team liquidated. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vic_Lee_Racing , https://1990sbtcc.com/2026/10/26/the-scandal-that-ended-a-championship-winning-team/
- First car: ex-Ravaglia M3 bought from Bigazzi. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-2005/80/start-something-big/

## Prodrive (Mobil BMW Team)
- Founded 1984 by Ian Parry and David Richards, Banbury. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prodrive
- "works assisted Prodrive team from Banbury"; Palmer in car 1, second car shared by Danner, Soper, Weaver. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/march-1991/25/british-touring-car-championship-3/
- Pirelli tyres, power deficit early in 1991. Nov 1991 article above.
- Doubt: a dealer advert calls a 1991 Prodrive/Soper M3 a "Mobil 1" car, so Mobil on the works car looks right; Sytner also had Mobil 1 backing in his Prodrive-run 1988 days.
- Malcolm Swetnam had been Prodrive team manager before joining Trakstar (~1989), not 1991. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/october-2020/94/cat-rouse-when-robb-gravett-and-the-trakstar-team-took-on-the-btcc

## Dave Cook Racing (Vauxhall Dealersport)
- Dave Cook Racing Services, Sheriff Hutton, Yorkshire, founded 1983; works Cavalier contract from 1991. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Cook_Racing
- 1991: new cars for Cleland (#5) and Allam (#6); Allam's car written off at Donington; Bob Berridge third car (#56) last two meetings. https://supertouringregister.com/notes/DCRSCavaliers/
- Dunlop tyres in 1991 (Dunlop man Tony Gilhome). Vauxhall's Mike Nicholson (died 2025 aged 81) moved from rally co-driving into Vauxhall motorsport management around 1990 and led the BTCC link; exact 1991 title unconfirmed. https://www.autosport.com/btcc/news/obituary-vauxhall-motorsport-boss-and-successful-co-driver-mike-nicholson-dies-aged-81/10709844/ , https://dirtfish.com/?p=131020
- Engine builder for 1991 not found (Swindon appears only for later cars).

## Andy Rouse Engineering (Kaliber ICS Team Toyota)
- Founded 1981 after Broadspeed; 18 staff in Solihull in 1989; Vic Drake on engines. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/page/january-1989/33 , https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Rouse
- 1991 cars "operated from Rousesport's Coventry workshops" (Rouse, Gary Ayles). https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/march-1991/25/british-touring-car-championship-3/
- Alan Gow worked for Rouse in 1989 (later TOCA boss); 1991 role unknown. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/august-2005/80/start-something-big/
- TOM'S GB only took over Toyota from 1993. https://supertouringregister.com/notes/ToyotaCarinaE/

## Trakstar (Shell Team Trakstar Ford)
- Founded 1989 by Robb Gravett and Mike Smith (TV presenter). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robb_Gravett
- Ford Competitions Manager Peter Ashcroft offered the works Ford deal in July 1990. 1991 car on Sapphire 4x4 shell, Mountune engine, Yokohama tyres, ~£150,000 per car, three cars planned (Gravett, Smith, customer Sean Walker). https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/page/may-1991/28
- Rod Vickery, Marketing Director, ex saloon car team manager. https://www.motorsportmagazine.com/archive/article/march-1991/25/british-touring-car-championship-3/
- Earlier staff (RS500 era): Malcolm Swetnam team manager (left early 1990), Ron Meadows (pit crew, later Mercedes F1), Brian Walsh (shell builder, Yorkshire). Oct 2020 article above.

## Graham Goode Racing
- Graham Goode Performance Centre founded 1976, Leicestershire; Goode ex-Broadspeed engineer. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graham_Goode_(racing_driver)
- Middlehurst 9th in 1991 with the NA 16-valve Sierra, car #99. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andy_Middlehurst

## Pyramid Motorsport
- 1990: BMW dealers Godfrey Hall, John Clark and Nick Whale ran three cars as Pyramid (forum claim via search, page blocked: https://tentenths.com/forum/showthread.php?p=2620947). 1991: Sytner most of the season, then David Leslie; Matt Neal debut at Silverstone round 7. https://1990sbtcc.com/2021/06/03/looking-back-at-1991-part-2/
- Sytner retired at end of 1991 for his dealer group. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Sytner

## Janspeed (Nissan Racing with Castrol)
- Janspeed Engineering Ltd, Salisbury, founded October 1962 by Hungarian emigre János Ódor. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janspeed
- Missed start of 1991; one car for Keith Odor, chassis 2 for Julian Bailey in the last three meetings; David Brown gearbox. https://supertouringregister.com/register/vehicle/183/

## Techspeed (Midland Auto Trader)
- Tech-Speed founded 1984; built and prepared BMW M3s for teams in the early 1990s; current principal Marvin Humphries (not 1991). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tech-Speed_Motorsport
- Nick Whale's car M3 1165 (ex Godfrey Hall), Midland Autotrader backing. https://www.classicdriver.com/en/car/bmw/m3/1990/884838
- John Llewellyn took Nick Baird's car at Brands Hatch. https://1990sbtcc.com/2021/06/03/looking-back-at-1991-part-2/

## Brodie Brittain Racing
- Base Brackley; turbo specialists; set up by Brodie and driver-engineer Ken Brittain, first BTCC car a Starion Turbo. https://www.autosport.com/general/news/obituary-national-racer-and-former-williams-director-dave-brodie/10686374/ , https://www.evo.co.uk/features/206237/my-life-cars-david-brodie-racer-and-bbr-co-founder
- March 1991 preview says workshop in Bracknell (likely error). Sapphire planned RWD, raced 4WD, debut Brands Hatch. https://1990sbtcc.com/2021/06/03/looking-back-at-1991-part-2/

## Others
- Mitsubishi: John McGuire/Maguire Racing, works backed Starion 1990, Lancer then Galant 1991 for Mark Hales (born 1950). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Hales
- HWR Motorsport: founded 1978 by Jeff Wilson and Brian Holliday; Tony Bardy second driver at round 12 (search result, unverified). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeff_Wilson_(racing_driver)
- Drambuie Racing: Ian Forrest, four BTCC seasons in private M3s said to be Prodrive-prepared. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Forrest
- R&D Motorsport: BMW 318is for Angus MacKay (first two rounds). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_MacKay_(racing_driver)
