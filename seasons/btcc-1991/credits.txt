Car images: Team Principal Mods (own work), same licence as the pack.
Cover photo: Silverstone 1991, Sutton Images / Motorsport Images, via Alamy (3ABP9N9). Bought licence, not covered by the pack licence.

Main menu background (assets/title_bg.png):
  The same Silverstone 1991 photo (Sutton Images / Motorsport Images,
  Alamy 3ABP9N9, bought licence, not covered by the pack licence).
  Moved right in a 16:9 frame; the extra pit building, crowd and wall on
  the left are AI-rendered (ChatGPT Images). The cars are the original
  photo. BTCC 1991 lettering made for this pack (Arvo, SIL OFL 1.1).

Season research sources: en Wikipedia ("1991 British Touring Car Championship").
See seasons/btcc-1991/research/season.md in the project repository for details.

Track maps and track geometry:
  Map data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the
  Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0 - https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
  Brands Hatch Indy is today's layout. Silverstone GP 1991, Snetterton
  1990 and Oulton Park 1975 are traced from old maps and imagery, on top of
  OpenStreetMap; the other circuits follow today's OpenStreetMap lines.
