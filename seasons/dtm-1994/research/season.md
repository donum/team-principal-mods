# DTM 1994 – research dossier

11th DTM season and the second under Class 1. There were 10 championship events × 2 heats = 20 races (10 Apr Zolder – 9 Oct Hockenheim), plus two non-championship "ITR Gold Cup" events at Mugello (22 May) and Donington (17 Jul). Champion: **Klaus Ludwig** (AMG-Mercedes C-Klasse), 222 pts, his third title. Race wins: Alfa Romeo 11, Mercedes 9. No team or manufacturer tables were found.

## Regulations
- Class 1 rules were unchanged from 1993 ("Il regolamento era uguale a quello del 1993", it.wikipedia): max 6 cylinders / 2.5 l / 4 valves per cylinder, production-derived engine block, and free aero below the wheel centreline. 4WD, traction control, ABS and electronic differentials were permitted. Purpose-built suspension only became allowed from 1995 (en.wikipedia "Class 1 Touring Cars").
- **No Class 2 entries in 1994.** The 2000 Cup was dropped and every listed entry is Class 1 (incl. old Group A M3 / 190E Evo II / Mustang).
- **Points** (each heat, top 10): 20-15-12-10-8-6-4-3-2-1, no extra points (en.wikipedia).
- **Weights**: not found. de.wikipedia gives 1,040–1,060 kg for the Calibra (1993–96).
- **Mandatory pit stop: NOT verified.** No source checked (de/en/it.wikipedia, netzwerkeins, autosportworld results) mentions a compulsory stop in 1994. Heat lengths (~100 km, ~40 min) and winning times give no evidence of one. Treat as "no mandatory stop" unless a period rulebook or magazine says otherwise.
- Fuel samples were checked: Nannini was disqualified from Donington heat 2 after the "obligatorische Benzinprobe" (mandatory fuel test) (de.wikipedia Calibra).

## Weekend format
- Qualifying set the heat 1 grid. As in 1993 there was no qualification race. The heat 2 grid followed the heat 1 result, with retirements at the back (de.wikipedia Calibra: Reuter started Donington heat 2 last after retiring in heat 1, and still won).
- Two heats of about 95–104 km. Exceptions per autosportworld: Nürburgring 2 June heat 1 had 19 laps / 58 km, and AVUS heat 1 had 33 laps / 87 km.

## Teams / people (documented)
- Alfa Corse (Larini, Nannini, Buttiero, then Modena from R8; Sullivan for the NC events), led by Giorgio Pianta (netzwerkeins). Alfa satellites: Schübel Engineering (Danner, Francia, Nissen), Jägermeister-Schübel (Bartels), FNAC-Schübel (Wallace), Engstler, Giudici.
- Mercedes: motorsport chief Norbert Haug. AMG (Affalterbach) ran two works pairs, "Tabac-Original Sonax" (Asch, Schneider) and "D2 Privat Team" (Ludwig, Lohr), plus Promarkt-Zakspeed (Thiim, van Ommen). 190E Klasse 1 privateers were Persson and the DTM-Junior-Team.
- Opel: sport chief Walter Treser. Team Joest ran 3 Calibras: Reuter, Rosberg, Louis Krages as "John Winter" (de.wikipedia Calibra).

## Storylines
- Mercedes debuted the C-Class. Thiim gave it its first win at Hockenheim R2, and Ludwig's consistency won the title, clinched at Singen.
- Alfa won 11 races but split points between Larini and Nannini. Modena arrived late and won both AVUS heats. Nissen took his first win at the Norisring.
- Opel returned. The Calibra was fast in qualifying but held back by brake and technical problems. Its only win came at the non-championship Donington heat 2. Winter's Calibra burned out at AVUS.
- Mugello and Donington ran outside the championship as the ITR Gold Cup, a precursor of the 1995 ITC.

## Unverified / gaps
- Circuit lengths: left empty. Lap counts and km come from autosportworld (rounded km) and were not cross-checked with a second source.
- The layout of the Nürburgring round on 2 June is unclear: 19 laps = 58 km and 32 laps = 97 km imply ~3.0 km. The reasons heat 1 there and AVUS heat 1 were shorter are unknown.
- Mandatory pit stop: no evidence found (see above).
- Minimum weights, power of the Calibra and the E36 M3s, and engines of the E36 M3s are unknown.
- Tyre suppliers for Alfa, Opel and the privateers are unknown. The Zakspeed/Michelin claim rests on a single source.
- Birth dates missing: Wolfgang Schrey, Karsten Molitor, Fritz Huber, Rüdiger Seyffarth.
- Round-attendance conflicts between de.wikipedia and en.wikipedia (Schmickler, Francia, Feucht, Engstler, Struwe, Huber) were resolved with autosportworld; noted per entry.
- Neil Cunningham's nationality: Wikidata says NZ, de.wikipedia says AUS.
- Final date: de.wikipedia's intro says 4 Oct; 9 Oct was used.
- Team bases are not documented except AMG (Affalterbach). Other team managers are unknown.
