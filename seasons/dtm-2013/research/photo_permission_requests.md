## Granted

**Dennis (inmotion-photos.com).** Email reply on 11 November 2026, from
dennis@inmotion-photos.com:

> if you credit me and add backlinks like you said you can use my DTM photos
> from my website.

- Covers: the DTM photos on his website. Not limited to a list of shots.
- Conditions: credit by name, plus backlinks to his website. Put both in
  `credits.txt` and on the Workshop page for every photo used.
- Not asked yet: full-size files, and the exact name he wants in the credit
  ("Dennis de Wolde / InMotion Photos", as in his watermark, until he says
  otherwise).
- The photos are saved locally and sorted by team in `inmotion_photos.md`.
- Keep the original email. It is the only record of the permission.

**Not covered here:** the six BMW press photos (MTEK and Schnitzer pit stops,
the BMW Welt presentation shots used as BMW HQ). BMW marks them free for
editorial use. A game mod isn't editorial use, so for a pack they need BMW's
press office, or they stay local only like the menu background (see
`LICENSE-CONTENT.md`).

**Check before sending:**
- *Parts Boutique:* the file name says "moscow-2014". If it's a 2014 photo, the
  car is the 2014 RS 5 and the pick should be replaced, not requested.
- *portugalmotorsport:* the title is "Audi Communications Motorsport", so it's
  likely an Audi press photo posted again. Ask Audi, or drop it.
