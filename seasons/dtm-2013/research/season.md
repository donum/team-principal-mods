# 2013 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 27th DTM season and the 14th since 2000. Ten rounds from 5 May to 20 Oct, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- New venue: Moscow Raceway (Volokolamsk). Valencia and the Munich show event were dropped.
- 22 cars:
  - BMW: 8 cars; MTEK joined.
  - Audi: 8 cars.
  - Mercedes: 6 cars; Persson Motorsport dropped.
- Champion: **Mike Rockenfeller** (Audi Sport Team Phoenix, Audi RS 5 DTM), 142 pts, ahead of Farfus (116) and Spengler (82). He clinched the title at Zandvoort, the penultimate round.
- Manufacturers: BMW 369, Audi 347, Mercedes 266. Teams' title: Audi Sport Team Phoenix (161).
- First season since 2005 without a female driver.

## Regulations (changes for 2013)
- **DRS** (Drag Reduction System):
  - The rear-wing flap opens to 15°.
  - Allowed if a driver is within 2.0 s of the car ahead at the control line, once per lap at any point on the track (de.wiki).
  - Not allowed on the first 3 and last 3 laps, or for 3 laps after a safety car.
  - Not used at Zandvoort for safety reasons (de.wiki 2014). DTM states up to about 10 km/h more top speed.
- **Option tyre:** a softer Hankook compound, 1–1.5 s per lap faster but quicker to degrade. One set per driver for the race, and it must be used once.
- **Pit stops:** still two mandatory stops. The pit-window rule was dropped. No stops on the first 3 or last 3 laps. A stop forced by damage, for example a puncture, does not count.
- **Weight:** 1,110 kg including driver (en.wiki; was 1,100). Rear tyre widened from 310 to 320 mm (de.wiki).
- **Qualifying:** knockout Q1–Q3 plus a single-lap Q4 shootout of the top 4. This was the last year of the shootout (de.wiki 2014).
- **Points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the top 10.
- Audi renamed the A5 DTM as the **RS 5 DTM**. Cars were otherwise technically unchanged.

## Cars and engines
- **BMW M3 DTM (E92):** P66 V8, 480 hp, 1,110 kg.
- **Audi RS 5 DTM:** 4.0 V8.
- **DTM AMG Mercedes C-Coupé:** 4.0 V8, 1,110 kg.
- All three use Hewland gearboxes and Hankook tyres.

## Storylines
- Rockenfeller was consistent, with wins at Brands Hatch and Moscow and 6 podiums.
- Farfus won 3 times (Hockenheim, Oschersleben, Zandvoort).
- **Norisring:** Ekström crossed the line first but was disqualified for a parc-fermé breach (a water bottle). The DMSB upheld the ruling, nobody was promoted, and the race officially has no winner.
- Timo Glock, in his rookie year after F1, won the Hockenheim finale.
- Wittmann was promoted from BMW test driver, and Wehrlein and Juncadella joined from F3.
- Ralf Schumacher retired just before the season and Coulthard left. Jamie Green moved from Mercedes to Audi.
