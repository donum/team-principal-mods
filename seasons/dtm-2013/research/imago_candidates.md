# DTM 2013 team pictures: imago candidates

Picked from a sweep of every imago result for "dtm" dated 2013 (13,399 captions), then checked by eye
as thumbnails. Nothing here has been checked at full size yet.

**Licensing:** these are rights-managed editorial images. The standard imago licenses don't cover a mod.
Treat this as a list of shots to ask about, not shots we can use. The credit on each detail page names
the photographer or agency to ask.

**Worth knowing:**
- One photographer (credited "mspb Jerri Andre") covers nearly the whole grid at Zandvoort. There is a
  matching head-on track shot of almost every car (14654486 to 14654505) and garage shots of eight cars
  (14654511 to 14654518). The same photographer shot four Mercedes garages at the Norisring (13980643 to
  13980646). One request to them could cover a large part of this list.
- The pit stop shots come from three series: the Hockenheim opener (13533003 to 13533538), Spielberg
  (13727866 to 13727874) and the finale (14814519 to 14814525). They show the whole crew, and the car is
  often half hidden.
- Off-track material is thin for the BMW teams. RBM, RMG and MTEK have no team boss shots and no real
  garage shots, so those lists use BMW-wide shots (trucks, Jens Marquardt).
- Phoenix has the most material. A "Phoenix Team Shooting" series (14626265 to 14626321) shows the
  workshop, offices and engineers. It's the only real HQ material in the whole sweep.

Categories: Track = car on track, Pit stop = car being serviced, Garage = car in the box,
Paddock = trucks or team area, HQ = factory or office, People = boss or engineer.
