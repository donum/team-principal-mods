# ADAC Formel Masters 2013 (Formel ADAC)

Sixth season of the ADAC's entry-level single-seater series. Lowest feeder series in the pack.

## Car
- One-make: Dallara monocoque ("Formel ADAC" car; Commons calls it Dallara Formulino), Volkswagen 1.6-litre FSI engine, 145 hp (official ADAC regulations page). Dunlop tyres (en Wikipedia series article).
- Car price 49,900 euros, ordered from ADAC Motorsport in Munich (regulations page; text written for 2012, still on the site in 2013).

## Format
- 8 race weekends, 3 races each, 24 races. Practice and qualifying Friday, race 1 Saturday, races 2 and 3 Sunday (2013 site calendar shows Fri-Sun events; 2014 timetable confirms the pattern).
- All 8 weekends on the ADAC GT Masters bill (same dates as 2013 ADAC GT Masters); German F3 (ATS Formel 3 Cup) also on the same bill.
- Points, races 1 and 2: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- Points, race 3: 15-10-8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1.
- No bonus points for pole or fastest lap (en Wikipedia series article). Picariello's total of 388 matches his race results with this scale exactly, so no hidden bonus.
- Prize fund 100,000 euros; top three get an ATS Formel 3 Cup test (regulations page).
- Team championship also run (Mücke 633, Neuhauser 453, Schiller 373, Lotus 320, KUG 246, JBR 62, KSW 15, HS Engineering 2; official site).

## Grid
- 23 drivers started at least one race, 8 teams.
- Cars per weekend: 21 at rounds 1-3, 19 at round 4, 20 at round 5, 21 at round 6, 20 at round 7, 21 at round 8 (counted from the entry list).
- Announced but did not start: Tina Arbinger (Rennsport Rössler) and David Detmers (Mücke) had 2013 driver portraits on the official site but no results.

## Age rules
- "Youngsters from the age of 15 upwards" (official regulations page). Exact cut-off date and upper limit not found; the 2013 sporting regulations PDF was linked but is not archived.

## Champion
- Alessio Picariello (BEL, ADAC Berlin-Brandenburg e.V. / Mücke Motorsport), 388 points, 12 wins from 24 races. Title clinched at Slovakia Ring (round 7). Günther second (240), Kremer third (227).
- Winners: Picariello 12, Günther 2, Beer 2, Dontje 2, Kremer, Grapp, Visser, F. Schiller, Dienst, Boschung 1 each. Boschung's win (Slovakia race 3) came after Picariello was penalised.

## Later careers (as of 2026 sources)
- No 2013 driver has started a Formula One race.
- Marvin Dienst: raced in DTM in 2021 (briefly); WEC and ELMS race winner.
- Ralph Boschung: several Formula 2 seasons from 2017; named Audi F1 development driver in February 2026.
- Maximilian Günther: Formula E driver from 2018-19.
- Others (Visser, Jensen, Beer, Picariello, Dontje, F. Schiller, Schramm) went on to F3, W Series or GT/sportscar racing; details not checked in depth.

## Data notes and uncertainties
- Points: the official ADAC standings (archived Oct and Dec 2013) match de Wikipedia. en Wikipedia has wrong totals for Dienst (171), Dontje (137), Boschung (122) and O'Keeffe (34). Official values used.
- Recomputing points from the Wikipedia race-by-race grid gives small differences for Beer (225 vs 217), Dontje, Jensen, F. Schiller, O'Keeffe and Kox. Probably penalties or grid errors; official totals used.
- Rookie flags are based on the 2012 entry lists on en/de Wikipedia. Hannes Utsch is not listed for 2012, but his official driver ID sits among 2012 drivers, so his rookie status is uncertain.
- Birth dates come from the official 2013 ADAC driver portraits, cross-checked with Wikipedia where articles exist. Boschung's date is from Wikipedia only. Herzog and Lenerz dates unknown.
- Team colours: only Mücke is photo-confirmed. Lotus black/gold is inferred from the same team's 2013 F3 cars. Other teams left empty.
- Callan O'Keeffe is listed as South African by the official site and Wikipedia.
