# German F3 2013 (ATS Formel 3 Cup)

The 2013 ATS Formel 3 Cup was the German Formula 3 championship (11th season under the "F3 Cup" name, 39th German F3 season by de.wikipedia's count). Organiser: Formel-3-Vereinigung e.V. (F3V), Bitburg. Title sponsor ATS (wheels). SONAX sponsored the rookie cup ("SONAX Rookie-Pokal"); DEKRA logos sit on every number board.

## Format

- 9 race weekends, 3 races each (27 planned). Race 2 at Nürburgring round 3 (24h support) was cancelled after fog, so 26 races were held.
- 8 of 9 rounds in Germany. Spa-Francorchamps (BEL) was the only foreign round.
- Support package: 6 rounds on the ADAC Masters Weekend (ADAC GT Masters, ADAC Formel Masters, ADAC Procar). Round 3 supported the 24 Hours Nürburgring. Rounds 5 (Lausitzring) and 8 (Oschersleben) supported the DTM.
- Race 2 grid: top 8 of race 1 reversed (racecar.com report from Lausitzring). Race 3 appears to start from a second qualifying order (Wikipedia lists a separate race 3 pole); not confirmed in a primary rule text.

### Classes

- **Cup** (main title): en.wikipedia says only 2008-2011 cars with the Volkswagen Power Engine were eligible. de.wikipedia says all drivers scored Cup points, and the published standings do give Cup points to Trophy drivers (Balthasar 10th with 54). Treat the overall table as open to all.
- **Trophy**: older cars (2002-2011 chassis) without the VW Power Engine (Mercedes, Opel/OPC-Challenge, older VW). Mostly gentleman drivers and young Germans on a budget.
- Also a SONAX Rookie-Pokal (won by Kirchhöfer) and a DMSB ranking for German licence holders (Kirchhöfer).

## Field size

- 30 drivers started at least one race (17 in Cup-class cars, 13 Trophy; Iannaccone drove both).
- 14 entrant names (13 real outfits; ADAC Team Nordbayern was a TTCmotorsport-run entry scored separately).
- Car count per weekend roughly 17-21 (estimate from rounds data, not counted from start lists).
- All cars Dallara (F302 to F311). Cup cars: VW Power Engine (Volkswagen, built by Spiess per general knowledge, unverified). Trophy: Mercedes (HWA-built F3 engine, general knowledge, unverified), Opel OPC-Challenge, older VW.

## Results

- **Champion: Marvin Kirchhöfer** (GER, Lotus/Motopark), 511 points, 13 wins and 25 podiums in 26 races, rookie in his first F3 year.
- 2nd Artem Markelov 339, 3rd Emil Bernstorff 334 (Lotus 1-2-3).
- Trophy champion: Sebastian Balthasar (GU-Racing, Mercedes), 415 Trophy points.
- Teams: Lotus 518, Van Amersfoort 341, Performance Racing 340.
- Race winners: Kirchhöfer 13, Bernstorff 6, Markelov 2, Menezes 2, Bryant-Meisner 2, Jäger 1.

## Later careers / DTM links

- **Matteo Cairoli** (ADM, 7th) is the only driver from this field found with DTM starts: en.wikipedia lists him in the 2026 DTM with Emil Frey Racing (Ferrari). Not a 2013-era DTM link.
- Checked en.wikipedia for Kirchhöfer, Markelov, Bernstorff, Menezes, Bryant-Meisner, Jäger, Jeffri, Mettler, Sylvest, Balthasar, Höher, Zlobin: no DTM.
- Kirchhöfer went on to GP3 and later McLaren GT factory driver. Markelov to GP2/F2. Menezes won LMP2 at Le Mans 2016 (general knowledge).

## Links to DTM makers and teams in 2013

- **Shared weekends:** rounds 5 (Lausitzring, 14-16 June) and 8 (Oschersleben, 13-15 Sept) were on the DTM bill.
- **Engines:** Volkswagen powered the Cup cars. VW was not a DTM maker in 2013 (its group brand Audi was). Mercedes F3 engines were used by several Trophy cars (Balthasar, Vier, Zeller, Amweg). Mercedes engines are usually built by HWA, which ran the Mercedes DTM programme. This HWA link is general knowledge, not confirmed by a 2013 source. Opel OPC-Challenge engines were also used; Opel had left the DTM by 2013.
- **Lotus / Motopark:** the top team ran under the Lotus name (Lotus F1-style black and gold). No 2013 DTM link. Mücke Motorsport (Mercedes DTM team in 2013) was not in German F3 in 2013.
- No BMW or Audi factory link to any 2013 German F3 entry was found.

## Gaps and conflicts

- Aberer nationality: AUT (en.wikipedia, formel3guide) vs SUI (de.wikipedia). Used austrian.
- Vier entrant split: en.wikipedia puts all rounds under ADAC Team Nordbayern; de.wikipedia splits rounds 1 and 4 to TTCmotorsport.
- Chassis: de.wikipedia shows all Cup cars as "Dallara F311"; en.wikipedia and formel3guide give F308/F310/F311 with serials. Used the serial numbers.
- Team bases: only Motopark (Oschersleben), Van Amersfoort (Huizen) and Jo Zeller (Oetwil am See) are given as towns; others are country only.
- Liveries: read from one photo per car; teams with mixed cars are noted in german_f3_teams.csv.
- Exact Race 3 grid rule and race lengths not confirmed from the regulations.
