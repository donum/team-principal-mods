# 2013 FIA Formula 3 European Championship

Research dossier for the DTM 2013 season pack. The series becomes a feeder series in the game.

## Format

- 10 rounds, 30 races. 3 races each weekend (usually races 1 and 2 on Saturday, race 3 on Sunday; Monza was 1 + 2).
- Car: Dallara F312 for everyone. Hankook tyres. Engines: Mercedes, Volkswagen, ThreeBond Nissan (T-Sport only).
- Points, every race the same: 25, 18, 15, 12, 10, 8, 6, 4, 2, 1 for the top 10.
- No pole or fastest-lap bonus points appear in the Wikipedia scoring table. Not cross-checked against the 2013 sporting regulations.
- Monza race 3 was red-flagged after half distance in heavy rain. Half points were awarded.
- Guest drivers (Kvyat, Cassidy, Ed Jones) could not score points. Their results were skipped, so other drivers still scored their finishing position's points (inferred from the standings table, not a stated rule).
- Ravenol Team Trophy: before each round, each team nominated two drivers to score team points.

## Field

- FIA season entry list (6 March 2013): 30 named cars plus one TBA (Romeo Ferraris #22).
- 39 drivers started at least one race: 36 classified in the championship plus 3 guests.
- 12 teams.

## Result

- Champion: Raffaele Marciello (Prema Powerteam), 489.5 points, 13 wins. He was runner-up in 2012.
- Runner-up: Felix Rosenqvist (kfzteile24 Mücke Motorsport), 457 points, 10 wins. Also runner-up in 2012.
- 3rd: Alex Lynn (Prema Powerteam), 339.5, 3 wins.
- Other winners: Lucas Auer, Harry Tincknell, Pascal Wehrlein (1 each), guest Daniil Kvyat (1, Zandvoort race 1).

## Drivers who later raced in DTM

From each driver's Wikipedia career summary.

- Pascal Wehrlein: Mercedes, 2013 (Mücke), 2014-2015 (HWA), 2018 (HWA). DTM champion 2015. He drove only round 1 of F3 in 2013 before his DTM debut.
- Lucas Auer: Mercedes 2015 (ART), 2016 (Mücke), 2017-2018 (HWA). BMW 2020 (RMR). Mercedes-AMG 2021 onward (Team Winward, GT3 era).
- Tom Blomqvist: BMW 2015-2017 (BMW Team RBM).
- Felix Rosenqvist: Mercedes 2016 (Mercedes-Benz DTM Team ART), after starting the year as reserve.
- Antonio Giovinazzi: Audi 2015 (Audi Sport Team Phoenix), 2 races.
- Nick Cassidy: 2019 (Lexus Team KeePer TOM'S, guest entry), Ferrari 2021-2022 (AlphaTauri AF Corse, GT3 era).
- Ed Jones: signed by Audi (WRT) for 2020 but replaced by Harrison Newey at the start of the season because of COVID-19 travel limits. Whether he started any DTM race is unclear.
- None found for Marciello, Lynn, Tincknell, King, Sims, Derani, Müller, Kvyat or the rest. Only the drivers' en.wikipedia pages were checked.

## 2013 links to DTM teams and makers

- Mücke Motorsport ran both series in 2013: Mercedes DTM cars and the kfzteile24 F3 team. Wehrlein moved from the F3 team to the DTM team after round 1. Michael Lewis took his F3 seat.
- Daniel Juncadella, 2012 F3 champion with Prema, moved to Mercedes in the 2013 DTM.
- 7 of the 12 teams used Mercedes engines. Not verified in this research: these were HWA-built engines (HWA also ran the Mercedes DTM programme).
- Carlin, ma-con and Van Amersfoort used Volkswagen engines. VW had no DTM programme. Its group sister Audi did.
- 7 of the 10 rounds supported the DTM (Hockenheim x2, Brands Hatch, Red Bull Ring, Norisring, Nürburgring, Zandvoort).
- Driver programmes: Kvyat was a Red Bull junior. Marciello was a Ferrari Driver Academy member. Neither link was re-checked in this research. No Audi, BMW or Mercedes junior status was confirmed for 2013.

## Gaps and uncertainties

- Team bases: ma-con, URD Rennsport, Jo Zeller Racing and Romeo Ferraris are known by country only. The T-Sport (Brackley) base comes from reports of other seasons. The EuroInternational (San Pietro Mosezzo) and Van Amersfoort bases are from current Wikipedia and may differ for 2013.
- Liveries: 4 teams have no colours found (URD, Jo Zeller, EuroInternational, Romeo Ferraris). Double R and Van Amersfoort colours are low confidence. The rest are estimated from Brands Hatch 2013 photos on Wikimedia Commons.
- Nationality choices: Serrallés is Puerto Rican but raced on a US licence. Goddard is Australian but raced on a British licence. Thompson is Irish but raced on a Japanese licence. Ed Jones is British-Emirati and was entered under the UAE flag.
- Chassis numbers per car exist on en.wikipedia (F312/0xx) but are not copied here.
