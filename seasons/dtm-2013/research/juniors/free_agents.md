# Extra free agents (DTM 2013 pack)

A small pool of drivers outside the covered feeder series (DTM, FIA F3 European, German F3, Carrera Cup Deutschland, ADAC Formel Masters) who could plausibly join a DTM team after March 2013. Data in `free_agents.csv`.

- `f1_starts` counts F1 starts before the 2013 season.
- `f1_years` lists F1 race seasons before 2013.
- `status_2013` is what the driver actually did in 2013.

## Suggested (6)

### Ex-F1

- **Vitaly Petrov.** Lost his Caterham seat for 2013 and had no race programme. Signed with Mercedes for DTM 2014 (Mücke Motorsport). The clearest real link.
- **Robert Kubica.** Tested a Mercedes DTM C-Coupé at Valencia on 24 January 2013 and did not rule out a race seat, but rallied with Citroën in 2013 and won WRC2. Raced DTM in 2020 with Orlen Team ART (BMW).
- **Heikki Kovalainen.** Caterham reserve at the start of 2013, then two late-season races for Lotus. Tested the BMW M4 DTM at the Lausitzring in August 2014. No DTM contract followed.
- **Lucas di Grassi.** Audi works driver in WEC in 2013. Tested the Audi RS 5 DTM at the Red Bull Ring in August 2013 but said his Audi future was in WEC. A plausible Audi pick in the game.

### Red Bull juniors

- **António Félix da Costa.** Red Bull junior, 3rd in Formula Renault 3.5 in 2013. Passed over for Toro Rosso and joined BMW in DTM 2014 to 2016.
- **Carlos Sainz Jr.** Red Bull junior in GP3 and part of Formula Renault 3.5 in 2013. No real DTM link. Plausible only if Red Bull drops him in the game.

## Not suggested, short reasons

- d'Ambrosio (Lotus reserve), Senna (Aston Martin WEC; declined a Mercedes DTM deal in 2009), Kobayashi (Ferrari WEC, back in F1 2014), Alguersuari (Pirelli tester), Buemi (Red Bull and Toyota ties), Heidfeld, Liuzzi, Klien, Chandhok, Karthikeyan: no DTM link, or too old.
- Klien did guest DTM races in 2021 (JP Motorsport McLaren), too late to matter.
- Williamson: dropped by Red Bull in June 2012, GP3 in 2013, no DTM link.
- Albon: dropped by Red Bull at the end of 2012. Raced DTM in 2021 for AF Corse, but he was only 16 in March 2013.
- Wackerbauer: Red Bull junior in 2012 only, minor results.

## Excluded

- Raced a covered series in 2013: Kvyat (FIA F3 guest with Carlin), Blomqvist and Lynn (FIA F3), Visser and Callan O'Keeffe (ADAC Formel Masters, both Red Bull juniors). Glock is already in DTM 2013. Charles Pic raced F1 in 2013.
- Not in the programme in 2012 or 2013: Mitch Evans (never a Red Bull junior), Gasly (from 2014), Ilott (2015). Wikipedia lists Lynn for 2014 only.
- Pedro de la Rosa: over 40 in 2013, outside the age range. Not researched further.

## Uncertain

- Albon's licence nationality in 2013 is not confirmed. He races under the Thai flag.
- Petrov: no 2013 activity in the part of the Wikipedia page that was read.
- The Kovalainen BMW test is "citation needed" on Wikipedia, but press reports confirm it.
