# Liveries for the teams that join later (for Pappydhorn12)

In a 2013 career these real teams join in later seasons. The pack shows
our own drafts for now (`art/cars.json`, cars with a `season`). These
are the cars to draw, like the 2013 sheets: one sheet per team, cars on
black, top to bottom, nose to the right.

The team image is the lower number (marked **team image**). Facts and
sources: `docs/teams/dtm_liveries.csv`. Colours are estimates from photos.

## Per car

**#2 Gary Paffett, Mercedes-AMG Team ART (2015)**
- Layout: Blue car; a yellow band sweeps from the rear bumper forward along the lower body to the front wheel arch; thin dark flow lines on the sill; rear wing black. Teammate Auer was completely different (pink BWT). Photo is a 2015 pre-season shot (location not stated).
- Other sponsors: Petronas (sill, rear wing endplate), Bosch (windscreen), DEKRA/ADAC number panel on front fender, H&R, Sandtler, Hankook, 'best of electronics!' slogan on sill
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2015/04/wpid-wp-1430206569410.jpeg (press photo, side view, not free) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2_Gary_Paffett_(27860732656).jpg (CC BY-SA 2.0, but 2016 car with the 2016 version of the Euronics livery)

**#22 Lucas Auer, Mercedes-AMG Team ART (2015)**
- Layout: Whole body pink with a water-drop print; rear wing black. Source image is the official launch render (top view), so side detail is partly unseen. Pink BWT scheme first ran at the Oschersleben test in April 2015.
- Other sponsors: Petronas (rear wing, sill), Bosch (windscreen), Hankook, DEKRA/ADAC number panel; a name starting 'EURODA..' on roof edge (unsure)
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2015/04/Auer_DTM_2015-600x338.jpg (render, not free)

**#16 Timo Glock, BMW Team RMR (2017)**
- Layout: All yellow; thin black pinstripe along waist line and lower sill; black diagonal BMW Motorsport band on bonnet; black splitter, sills and diffuser. Teammate Blomqvist white with M stripes. TCT says 2017 liveries were very similar to 2016; the 2016 and 2018 Commons photos show near identical yellow schemes.
- Other sponsors: Bosch (windscreen), Shell Helix Ultra (front), BMW Driving Experience (front fender), H&R (rear fender), Mahle (rear bumper), Puma (rear wing), Hankook, 'I love post' front plate
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2017/04/0441f26e-29b2-4e3f-b927-2bebda3597a5-orginal-600x338.jpg (2017 render, not free) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:16_Timo_Glock_(27860673816).jpg (CC BY-SA 2.0, clean side view, 2016 car) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2018_Deutsche_Tourenwagen_Masters,_Brands_Hatch_(45568478871).jpg (CC BY-SA 2.0, 2018)

**#31 Tom Blomqvist, BMW Team RMR (2017)**
- Layout: White car; M tricolour stripes run diagonally over bonnet and roof and onto rear wing; black sills and lower doors. M colours added for 40 years of BMW M. Teammate Glock yellow. No free 2017 photo found.
- Other sponsors: Bosch (windscreen), large H&R logo on door (read from render, check), DEKRA/ADAC, Shell (rear), Mahle (rear bumper), Puma (rear wing), Hankook
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2017/04/e408f03b-bb07-49b6-ad24-61a8555f8888-orginal-600x338.png (render, not free)

**#3 Paul di Resta, Mercedes-AMG Motorsport SILBERPFEIL Energy (2017)**
- Layout: White upper body; anthracite sills and lower front and rear; orange and red 'digital flow' streaks along the sill and rear lower edge (shared Mercedes 2017 design element); black rear wing with orange tip. Teammate Engel silver and anthracite. Team name SILBERPFEIL Energy was already used by HWA in 2015 and 2016.
- Other sponsors: Petronas (rear wing, sill), Remus (sill), H&R and Sandtler (rear bumper), Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/ADAC
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2017/04/C205_2017_Di_Resta_3-600x450.jpg (Daimler side render, not free)

**#63 Maro Engel, Mercedes-AMG Motorsport SILBERPFEIL Energy (2017)**
- Layout: Same template as di Resta but silver body with darker front nose; orange digital flow streaks low on the sides. Motorsport Week calls it black, silver and orange.
- Other sponsors: Petronas (rear wing, sill), Remus, H&R, Sandtler, Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/ADAC
- Photos: https://www.touringcartimes.com/app/uploads/2017/04/C205_2017_Engel_63-600x450.jpg (Daimler side render, not free) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Mattias_Ekstr%C3%B6m,_Maro_Engel,_DTM_Moscow_2017_(35723963680).jpg (CC BY 2.0, podium photo, shows sponsor logo only)

**#3 Paul di Resta, R-Motorsport (2019)**
- Layout: Grey body; thin light blue line traces bonnet edge, headlights, side skirt and rear arch; black sills and lower doors; black rear wing; orange mirrors at Hockenheim. Same grey scheme as teammate Dennis; the R-Motorsport II cars were black.
- Other sponsors: Vynamic (sill), R-Motorsport R logo (rear wing endplates), Bosch (windscreen), Hankook, DEKRA/DTM panel, Deutsche Post front plate
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Paul_di_Resta_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aston_Martin_Vantage_DTM_2019.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0, Norisring parc ferme)

**#76 Jake Dennis, R-Motorsport (2019)**
- Layout: Same grey template as di Resta; white mirrors at Hockenheim. Sponsors may have changed by round (unsure).
- Other sponsors: Vynamic (sill), 'MOTORSPORT' above windscreen, Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/DTM, Deutsche Post front plate
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jake_Dennis_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jake_Dennis_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP2.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0)

**#23 Daniel Juncadella, R-Motorsport 2 (2019)**
- Layout: Same line pattern as the grey cars but body black/anthracite; yellow mirrors at Hockenheim. Same scheme as teammate Habsburg.
- Other sponsors: Vynamic (sill), 'MOTORSPORT' above windscreen, Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/DTM, Deutsche Post front plate
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Daniel_Juncadella_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Daniel_Juncadella_@_Brands_Hatch_-_DTM.jpg (CC BY-SA 2.0)

**#62 Ferdinand Habsburg, R-Motorsport 2 (2019)**
- Layout: Same black scheme as Juncadella; orange mirrors at Hockenheim.
- Other sponsors: Vynamic (sill), 'MOTORSPORT' above windscreen, Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/DTM, Deutsche Post front plate
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferdinand_Habsburg_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ferdinand_Habsburg_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP2.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0)

**#21 Pietro Fittipaldi, Audi Sport Team WRT (2019)**
- Layout: Black car; lime band along the window line and beltline; lime slash stripe packs on front fenders and behind the door; lime mirrors. Teammate Aberdein same design with red-orange accents. Andrea Dovizioso drove this car at Misano.
- Other sponsors: Traxalt (bonnet), Akrapovic (sill), Audi Sport (rear wing), Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/DTM, WRT logos
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pietro_Fittipaldi_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pietro_Fittipaldi_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP2.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0)

**#27 Jonathan Aberdein, Audi Sport Team WRT (2019)**
- Layout: Same design as Fittipaldi with red-orange in place of lime: band along window line, slash stripes on fenders and rear door, red mirrors. The Wikipedia entry table is garbled about his number; photos and Audi media show 27.
- Other sponsors: Akrapovic (sill), Audi Sport (rear wing), Bosch, Hankook, DEKRA/DTM
- Photos: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jonathan_Aberdein_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP1.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Jonathan_Aberdein_2019_DTM_Hockenheim_(May)_FP2.jpg (CC BY-SA 4.0) | https://www.touringcartimes.com/img/2019/03/MG_2447.jpg (launch side view by Patrick Hecq, not free)

**#8 Robert Kubica, Orlen Team ART (2020)**
- Layout: White nose and front; red takes over from the door rearwards with a sharp diagonal split; thin white jagged lines over the red and red jagged lines on the white front. Single car team.
- Other sponsors: Benzina (sill), Orlen Oil Platinum (front bumper), Bosch (windscreen), Hankook, DEKRA/DTM, 'star' fuel brand (named in press release, placement unknown)
- Photos: https://cdn-images.motor.es/image/m/1320w/fotos-noticias/2020/03/robert-kubica-muestra-decoracion-bmw-m4-dtm-202065392-1583225953_1.jpg (launch photo, not free) | no free Commons photo found

## Notes

- The cars are newer models than the 2013 ones: Mercedes C-Coupe (2015,
  the old body), BMW M4 DTM and Mercedes-AMG C 63 DTM (2017), Aston
  Martin Vantage DTM and the 2019 Audi RS 5 DTM, BMW M4 DTM Turbo (2020).
- Free side-view photos on Wikimedia Commons (CC BY-SA, by Morio and
  others) exist for the Aston Martin, the 2019 Audi and the 2016 BMW and
  Mercedes; the file names are in the CSV.
- When a sheet arrives: add it to `art/drawings.json` and run
  `tools/car_drawings.py`. The drawing then replaces our draft.
