# DTM 2013 team pictures: InMotion Photos (Dennis de Wolde)

Permission granted on 11 November 2026 for all DTM photos on
https://inmotion-photos.com, with a credit and backlinks (see
`photo_permission_requests.md`). The watermark reads
"INMOTION-PHOTOS © DENNIS DE WOLDE".

**Only the 2013 album goes into this pack.** The other years are kept for
other seasons. 2012 fits the DTM 2012 pack.

**Worth knowing:**
- These are web sizes. Full-size files would have to come from Dennis.
- Every photo has his watermark in the bottom left corner. A crop that cuts it
  off still needs the credit.
- He shot from spectator areas (no accreditation). So it's all cars on track,
  no garages, pit stops or people. All 12 teams of 2013 appear.
