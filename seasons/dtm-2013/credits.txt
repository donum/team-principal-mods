DTM 2013 - Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters
Unofficial community season pack for Team Principal: A Racing Manager.

Created by Daniel Raude - Team Principal Mods
Content licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Tooling MIT.

Season research sources: de/en Wikipedia, the DTM fan wiki (dtm.fandom.com),
racingyears.com, crash.net, Wikidata.
Driver codes, sponsors and livery notes were checked against Andy
Blackmore's 2013 DTM Spotter Guide (v4, andyblackmoredesign.com /
SpotterGuides.com), used as a reference for facts only: no part of the
guide is included in the pack.
See seasons/dtm-2013/README.md in the project repository for details.

Car images (assets/Cars):
  Drawn by Pappydhorn12 and used with the artist's permission. All rights
  to these drawings stay with the artist; they are not covered by the
  pack's Creative Commons licences.

Team list icons (assets/logos):
  Each team is shown with its maker's emblem (Audi, BMW or Mercedes-Benz),
  as on the TV timing graphics. See the maker logos below.

Track maps (assets/tracks):
  Race-day illustrations of the 2013 venues, made in the race-track-themes
  project. The scenery is AI-rendered. The track surface, kerbs, pit lane
  and paddock layout are drawn over it from OpenStreetMap data (see the
  map data credit below).

Team dashboard backgrounds (assets/dashboard_bgs):
  Photos from the 2013 race weekends, cropped, extended to 16:9 and touched
  up with ChatGPT image editing (fences, watermarks and stray text removed;
  the car moved into the visible part of the dashboard). Edits of CC BY-SA
  photos are shared under CC BY-SA 2.0, the others under the pack's licence.
  Dennis de Wolde's photos are used with his permission; they keep their
  copyright and are not covered by the pack's licence.
