Season research sources: de/en Wikipedia, the DTM fan wiki (dtm.fandom.com),
racingyears.com, crash.net, Wikidata.
Driver codes, sponsors and livery notes were checked against Andy
Blackmore's 2013 DTM Spotter Guide (v4, andyblackmoredesign.com /
SpotterGuides.com), used as a reference for facts only: no part of the
guide is included in the pack.
See seasons/dtm-2013/README.md in the project repository for details.
