[h1]DTM 2013 - Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters[/h1]

An unofficial season pack for Team Principal. Run a team in the 2013 DTM season with the real teams, drivers, cars and tracks.

[h2]What is in the pack[/h2]
[list]
[*]11 teams and 22 drivers from the 2013 entry list, with their real car numbers.
[*]Audi, BMW and Mercedes-AMG as works partners. All three use a 4.0 litre V8.
[*]The 2013 calendar. Ten rounds at nine tracks, from Hockenheim in May to the Hockenheim finale in October.
[*]Tracks traced from OpenStreetMap, with the 2013 routes such as the Nürburgring sprint course and the Lausitzring short course.
[*]2013 rules. Knockout qualifying, DRS, no refuelling, two cars per team, points from 25 down to 1.
[*]Hankook tyres. A standard tyre and an option tyre, and both must be used in a dry race.
[*]Real team principals and technical staff.
[*]Starting sponsors from the 2013 liveries.
[*]Five real feeder series from 2013. FIA F3 European Championship, Porsche Carrera Cup Deutschland, ATS Formel 3 Cup, ADAC Formel Masters and the ADAC Procar series.
[*]Drawn cars, driver portraits, team photos as dashboard backgrounds and new menu music.
[/list]

Ratings and skills are estimates based on the 2013 results. They are not history.

[h2]Changes in 0.3[/h2]
[list]
[*]Retirements per track are fitted to the 2012 and 2013 races. About 14 in 100 starters retire, as in those seasons.
[*]Overtaking per track is fitted to the same races.
[*]Pit lane time loss per track, checked against TV timing and commentary.
[*]Pole position starts on the side seen in the 2013 TV starts.
[*]Rain chance per track from the climate in the race month.
[*]Tyre wear raised at Moscow, Brands Hatch and the Norisring, so most races are won on two stops.
[/list]

[h2]Changes in 0.2[/h2]
[list]
[*]Real team principals and technical staff.
[*]Five more driver portraits.
[/list]

[h2]Known limits[/h2]
[list]
[*]The Q4 shootout for the top four is not in the game. Qualifying is a normal knockout.
[*]The game can't require two pit stops. At Brands Hatch, the Norisring and Moscow some AI cars stop once or three times, most of all after a safety car.
[*]Track outlines are today's layouts and may differ in detail.
[/list]
