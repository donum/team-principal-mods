# 2020 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 9 weekends with 2 races each, so 18 races. The season ran from 1 August (Spa-Francorchamps) to 8 November (Hockenheim).
- The calendar was rebuilt around COVID-19:
  - Several circuits hosted back-to-back weekends: Lausitz Sprint then Lausitz Grand Prix, Nürburgring Grand Prix then Sprint, and Zolder twice.
  - Only Germany and the Benelux countries were visited.
  - The original 10-round, 20-race calendar was cancelled: Zolder (opener), Igora Drive, Anderstorp, Monza, Norisring, Brands Hatch and others.
- Only 2 makes, Audi and BMW, with 16 cars. Aston Martin and R-Motorsport did not return.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **René Rast** (Audi Sport Team Rosberg) with 353 points, ahead of Müller (330) and Frijns (279). His third title, decided in the last race.
  - Teams: Audi Sport Team Abt Sportsline
  - Manufacturers: Audi
- **Audi's last DTM season** as a works manufacturer.

## Regulations
- Same Class One base as 2019: 2.0 L I4 turbo engines and Hankook tyres.
- Changes for 2020:
  - **Push-to-pass doubled from 30 to 60 hp.** Base power about 580 hp + 60 hp.
  - **DRS use became unrestricted** (en.wiki wording).
  - **Fuel-mass flow** cut from 95 to 90 kg/h. The extra flow for push-to-pass rose from 5 to 10 kg/h.
  - **HYLO (High Yaw Lift-Off)** flaps added in front of the rear-wing pillars, to stop cars becoming airborne.
- Hankook's contract was extended to at least 2023. It beat bids from Continental, Dunlop, Michelin, Pirelli and Yokohama.
- **Format:** carried over from 2019. 55 min + 1 lap per race, with 1 mandatory pit stop. Separate qualifying before each race, with 3-2-1 qualifying points and 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the race.
- **BMW customer teams** were allowed, which brought ART back.
- **Teams' standings:** WRT ran 3 cars, but its team total (103) equals its best two cars per race. Driver sum is 115, which is consistent with 2 cars counting per team.

## Storylines
- **Audi exit:** announced on 27 April 2020, effective at the end of 2020. The stated focus was Formula E and customer racing, and the pandemic was cited. BMW criticised Audi for not informing it beforehand.
- **Aston Martin:** R-Motorsport announced on 24 January 2020 that it was leaving the DTM after one season. It had a provisional BMW engine deal for 2020. Aston Martin ruled out HWA running the cars.
- **Driver moves:**
  - Ed Jones (WRT) withdrew because of COVID-related travel problems, and Harrison Newey replaced him.
  - Spengler left for BMW in IMSA.
  - Eriksson went to the ADAC GT Masters.
  - Auer returned with BMW.
  - Former F1 driver Robert Kubica made his debut.
- **Duval** missed Zolder II because it clashed with Petit Le Mans. Benoît Tréluyer replaced him.
- **Title race:** Audi took 16 of 18 wins. BMW's two came from Auer (Lausitz GP race 2) and van der Linde (Assen race 2).
