DTM 2020 - Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters
Unofficial community season pack for Team Principal: A Racing Manager.

Created by Daniel Raude - Team Principal Mods
Content licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Tooling MIT.

Season research sources: de/en Wikipedia, racingyears.com, racer.com,
autosport.com, touringcartimes.com, Wikidata.
See seasons/dtm-2020/README.md in the project repository for details.

Tracks at Spa-Francorchamps and the Nürburgring Grand Prix circuit come
from the project's F1 2010-2013 base. Lausitzring Grand Prix, Assen and
Zolder use placeholder geometry from other tracks for now.

Sounds: the game's own sounds. No DTM 2020 engine recordings are included.
