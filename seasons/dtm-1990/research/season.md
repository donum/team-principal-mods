# DTM 1990: research dossier

The 7th DTM season had 11 rounds and 22 heats (10 in Germany, 1 in Belgium), from 1 Apr (Zolder) to 14 Oct (Hockenheim).
Hans-Joachim Stuck won the title (Audi V8 quattro, SMS) with 189 points, ahead of Cecotto (177) and Thiim (162).
It was Audi's first DTM season. Ford had left the series.

## Regulations
- **Base rules:** FIA Group A with ONS/ITR DTM rules, with more freedom for engines than plain Group A. Turbo engines were banned from 1990 [en.wiki].
- **Points:** 20-15-12-10-8-6-4-3-2-1 to the top 10 of each heat. Every result counted [de.wiki; TCR totals agree].
- **Minimum weights (TCR car sheet):**
  - BMW M3 Sport Evo and Mercedes 190E Evo: 1040 kg.
  - Audi V8: 1220–1250 kg. Competitors said 1250 kg was too low for the big car.
- **Success ballast ("handicap weight", TCR):**
  - The maximum was **100 kg**. At Wunstorf, Ludwig, Thiim, Cecotto and Soper all ran at the maximum.
  - Ballast came off again with poor results ("to lose 20 kgs for the next race").
  - Giroix ran 82.5 kg at Mainz.
  - Newcomers started without handicap weight (Norisring). This is the opposite of 1991, where newcomers started on the maximum.
  - The per-position scale is unknown.
- **Mid-season rule changes (TCR):**
  - Mercedes 190E Evo II was eligible from 1 May 1990. AMG first raced it at the Nordschleife (rd 7). The second AMG pair and Snobeck got it from rd 10, MS-Jet from rd 11.
  - Audi received **80 kg of extra ballast** for Diepholz (rd 9). **50 kg** of it was removed again before rd 10.

## Weekend format (TCR race pages)
- **Qualifying** set the grid. Roughly the top 16 went straight into the heats (Norisring: "first 16 getting direct access").
- **Qualification race** (8–22 laps, about 40–50 km) for everyone else. It was held at every round except the Nordschleife (rd 7).
- **Two heats** of about 100 km each.
- No planned pit stops are mentioned. Cars pitted only for damage. Spare cars were widely used for heat 2.

- Confirmed: Yokohama (Schnitzer/BMW), Dunlop (AMG, Ludwig is called a "Dunlop man", and Audi).
- Unknown: Bigazzi, Linder, Zakspeed BMW, Snobeck, MS-Jet, Opel and the privateers.

## Storylines
- **Audi debut:** one car for Stuck, run by SMS (Konrad Schmidt Motorsport). Röhrl joined from the Norisring and Jelinski was added for the finale.
- **BMW's new M3 Sport Evo** had seven engine failures at Zolder.
- **BMW's works effort was spread over many teams:** Schnitzer, Bigazzi, Linder and Zakspeed. Bigazzi took a 1-2-3 at the Eifel.
- **Mercedes struggled** with handicap weight until the Evo II arrived.
- **Opel's Omega** was withdrawn at Zolder and first raced at the AVUS.
- **Team tactics:**
  - BMW chief Kalbfell slowed Pirro so that Röhrl could win at the Nürburgring.
  - At the finale, all makes brought extra cars. Guest Michael Schumacher (AMG) took Cecotto out at the first corner, and Audi finished 1-2-3 twice to give Stuck the title.
- **Personnel changes:** Thomas Winkelhock was dropped by AMG after rd 6. Kris Nissen missed the rounds from rd 9 to "recover".
