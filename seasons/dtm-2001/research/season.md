# DTM 2001: research dossier

The second season of the revived DTM had 10 events with 2 races each (20 races): 8 in Germany, plus the A1-Ring (Austria) and Zandvoort (Netherlands). It ran from 22 Apr to 7 Oct, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
Bernd Schneider (D2 AMG-Mercedes) won the title with 161 points, ahead of Uwe Alzen (101) and Peter Dumbreck (88). He was the first driver to defend a DTM title.

## Regulations
- **Technical base:** unchanged from 2000.
  - 4.0 L naturally aspirated 90° V8 with 2 x 28 mm restrictors, rear-wheel drive.
  - Minimum weight 1080 kg including driver.
  - Dunlop control tyres.
  - See dtm-2000 for details.
- **Car spec:** year-old 2000-spec cars were allowed alongside 2001 cars.
  - Mercedes customers Persson and Rosberg ran 2000 CLKs.
  - Opel customers Phoenix #8, Euroteam and Mamerow ran 2000 Astras.
- **Engines:** the 2002 regulations mention 2 sealed engines per car per season (totalmotorsport, 2002). In 2003 the allowance was cut from 4 to 3 per two-car team. That implies 4 per pair in 2001–02.

## Weekend format and points (de.wiki "Reglement")
- **Friday:** a 15-minute roll-out and two 90-minute test sessions. These replaced the old 10-minute function test.
- **Saturday:** 1 hour of free practice, then **qualifying**: 45 minutes with a maximum of **12 laps** per driver. The fastest driver takes pole for the qualifying race.
- **Sunday, qualifying race (Qualifikationslauf):** a sprint of about 30–35 km that sets the main-race grid.
- **Between the races:** a 25-minute window in which teams may refuel, change tyres and repair cars.
- **Sunday, main race (Wertungslauf):** about 100 km with **1 mandatory pit stop, all 4 tyres changed, no refuelling**.
- **Starts:** rolling starts.
- **Points:**
  - Qualifying race: 3-2-1 to the top 3.
  - Main race: 20-15-12-10-8-6-4-3-2-1 to the top 10.
  - No dropped scores. (en.wiki's text "top 6" for the main race is wrong. Schneider's 161 = 11 QR points + 150 main-race points confirms the top-10 scale.)
- **Teams:** 2-car entries; Holzer was split into Holzer I and Holzer II. de.wiki also lists a manufacturers table (Markenwertung), but its official status in 2001 is unclear.

## Teams and people
- **Mercedes, HWA works cars:** D2 (#1/#2) and Warsteiner (#5/#6).
- **Mercedes customer teams:**
  - Manthey Racing (Eschmann) was new and ran 2001-spec cars.
  - Persson (Original-Teile) and Rosberg (Service 24h) ran year-old cars.
- **Audi:** Abt added a Junior team for rookies Ekström and Tomczyk.
- **Opel:** the cars ran under the OPC (Opel Performance Center) banner. Holzer ran 3 cars and Phoenix 2. Euroteam had 2 cars, and Mamerow ran a single privateer car.
- **Team principals:** not documented in the sources checked (see 2000 for names from general knowledge).

## Storylines
- **Schneider's title.** Schneider won 6 races (QR and main combined) and clinched the title at the A1-Ring (rd 8).
- **Audi's progress.** Aïello won both races at Nürburgring 1 and the main race at Nürburgring 2.
- **Rookies and first wins.**
  - Abt's Junior rookies scored early: Tomczyk was 2nd in the rd 2 QR, and Ekström 2nd in the rd 4 QR.
  - Dumbreck took his first win at Lausitz.
  - Mayländer won the finale for Manthey.
- **Disqualification:** Christian Abt was disqualified from the Nürburgring 2 main race.
- **Aïello's absence:** he missed the Sachsenring and was replaced by Kris Nissen. The reason is not documented.
- **Opel's season:** Opel won no race. Its best results were Winkelhock 4th at the Norisring and Reuter 4th at the A1-Ring and Hockenheim.
