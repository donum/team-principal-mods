# DTM 1992: research dossier

The 9th DTM season had 12 rounds and 24 heats (10 in Germany, 1 in Belgium, 1 in Czechoslovakia), from 5 Apr (Zolder) to 11 Oct (Hockenheim).
Klaus Ludwig won the title (Mercedes 190E Evo II, AMG) with 228 points, ahead of Thiim (192), B. Schneider (191) and Cecotto (185).
It was Mercedes' first DTM drivers' title, and the last season of the Group A-based DTM.

## Regulations
- **Base rules:** Group A-based ONS/ITR DTM rules (TCR: "the last under the same regulations as more or less started in 1986"). There were no new Evolution homologations.
- **Points:** 20-15-12-10-8-6-4-3-2-1 to the top 10 of each heat. Every result counted [de.wiki; TCR].
- **Manufacturers' points:** only the best-placed car of each make scored [de.wiki].
- **Minimum weights:**
  - Audi V8: 1300 kg [de.wiki Audi V8 DTM; TCR].
  - BMW M3 and Mercedes 190E: 980 kg [TCR car sheet].
- **Success ballast:** in use (TCR ruleset "DTM with weight penalties"). Examples: Thiim 30 kg at the Eifel, Ludwig 50 kg at Singen. The maximum and the scale are not documented.
- **Mid-season changes:**
  - **Nordschleife (rd 6):** the ONS gave BMW a larger front splitter and a higher rear wing ("balance of performance"). BMW also added bigger brake discs and Teves 4-channel ABS.
  - **Audi crankshaft:** Audi had twisted the series 90° crank into a 180° (flat-plane) layout. BMW protested, and the ONS ruled it legal, also on a later Mercedes protest. **After the Nordschleife round, the ONS appeal court declared it illegal.** Audi withdrew with immediate effect. It was short of the older 90° "Stufe 2c" engines and felt over-penalised. Audi's last race was rd 6, and it skipped 1993 [de.wiki Audi V8 DTM; TCR].

## Weekend format (TCR race pages)
- **Qualifying** set the grid. Non-qualifiers ran a qualification race of 8–22 laps.
- **No qualification race at:** Eifel (rd 2), AVUS (rd 4) and the Nordschleife (rd 6).
- **Two heats** of about 100 km each. Exceptions:
  - Norisring heat 1: 41 laps.
  - Brno heat 2: 16 laps.
- No planned pit stops are mentioned. Spare cars were widely used.

## Tyres
- **AMG: Bridgestone.** Bridgestone paid AMG "several millions". At the wet Eifel round, AMG still tried Pirelli and Michelin rain tyres.
- **Zakspeed Mercedes: Michelin** (Thiim "Michelin-shod").
- **Schnitzer BMW: Yokohama.**
- **Audi: Dunlop.**
- **Unknown:** Bigazzi, FINA, MS Racing, Linder, Isert, Valier, MM, the Mustangs and the other privateers.

## Storylines
- **Opel works team absent:** it was preparing the Calibra for 1993. The only Opel was Strycek's one-off Irmscher Omega at rd 6.
- **New and renamed teams:**
  - FINA Motorsport Team: Cecotto's semi-works BMW, based at the Nürburgring and managed by ex-Zakspeed man Günther Warthofer.
  - Diebels Zakspeed Team (Mercedes): Thiim moved there to make room for Rosberg at AMG.
  - MS Racing.
  - Manthey Racing.
  - Buffalo Boots Racing (Mustang).
  - BMW Sweden, VAC and Bervid (one-offs).
  - Jägermeister Linder Team.
- **Unitron Sports folded** after rd 8 (sponsor bankrupt). Nissen later moved to Isert.
- **Ellen Lohr won H1 at the Hockenheim short circuit (rd 5).** She is the only woman to win a DTM race.
- **Audi:** won only the wet Eifel H1, with an Audi 1-2-3 (Biela, Stuck, Jelinski). It then withdrew after rd 6 (see the crankshaft ruling above).
- **Mercedes team orders:** at the Nürburgring GPdT, Rosberg had to let Ludwig win. The crowd was angry.
- **Ludwig clinched the title** at the Hockenheim finale.
- **BMW's exit:** Ravaglia won both heats of the Hockenheim finale, the M3's farewell. BMW then left the DTM (withdrawal in Dec 1992, de.wiki).

## Unverified / gaps
- **Circuit lengths and distances:**
  - AVUS: 2.640 km (TCR) vs. 2.632 km implied by de.wiki's distance.
  - Wunstorf: de.wiki's distance (99.924 km) looks wrong.
  - Nürburgring rd 11 "short layout" (3.029 km): the exact name of the layout is not confirmed.
- **Brno:** why H2 ran only 16 laps is unknown.
- **Wayne Gardner:** TCR names "Frank Gardner" at rd 12, treated as a typo.
- **Birth dates missing:** Fred Räker, Siegfried Maier. Václav Bervid's 1973 birth date (Wikidata/de.wiki) makes him 19, which is plausible but unconfirmed.
- **Team bases:** Freilassing (Schnitzer), Affalterbach (AMG) and Niederzissen (Zakspeed) come from general knowledge, not from the sources above.
- **Manufacturers' standings:** de.wiki lists Ford with 1 point; TCR lists only Mercedes, BMW and Audi.
- **No team standings found.** A secondary site ranks AMG, Schnitzer, Zakspeed, Bigazzi in that order, without points.
- **Tyres:** missing for most teams.
- **Mustang and Omega:** power and weights unknown.
- **Simtek M3:** which team ran it is unknown.
