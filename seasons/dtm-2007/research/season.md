# 2007 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 10 rounds, one race each, from Hockenheim (22 Apr) to Hockenheim (14 Oct).
- Mugello returned in place of Le Mans. The Düsseldorf presentation was on 15 Apr.
- **Champion:** Mattias Ekström (Abt Sportsline Audi A4 DTM 2007), 50 points and 1 win. It was his 2nd title, after 2004.
  - Runner-up: Bruno Spengler, 47.
  - 3rd: Martin Tomczyk, 40.
- **Teams' title:** Audi Sport Team Abt Sportsline, 90 points.
- **Brand title:** Audi (de.wiki).
- Field: 20 cars per round. Each make ran 4 new cars plus older generations.
- Paul di Resta was a rookie in a two-year-old car. He briefly led the championship and still had a theoretical title chance late in the season.

## Regulations
- **Mercedes model change:** Mercedes switched to the W204 C-Klasse body, so the development restrictions were looser than in 2006.
- **Handicap weights:** extended to **every car generation separately**, so an older car could be heavier than a newer one (de.wiki DTM).
  - The result-based weight step was **7 kg**. From round 6 (Mugello) it was cut to **5 kg** (touringcartimes, Jul 2007).
  - Lausitz awarded half points and half weight steps (3.5 kg).
  - **Finale weights**, reset by the DMSB from the running order at lap 46 in Barcelona (touringcartimes):

    | Car | Weight |
    |---|---|
    | Mercedes 2007 | 1,075 kg |
    | Audi 2007 | 1,065 kg |
    | Mercedes 2006 | 1,060 kg |
    | Audi 2006 | 1,055 kg |
    | Mercedes 2005 | 1,055 kg |
    | Audi 2005 | 1,030 kg |

  - Car-article base weights conflict: Audi A4 1,070 kg (2007–08) and W204 1,040 kg (2007–08), both including driver.
- **New safety-car rule** (F1-style): the pit lane closed when the safety car came out and reopened once the field was lined up behind it. This affected Hockenheim, Lausitz, Norisring and Mugello. It was dropped for 2008.
- **Race:** about 160–170 km, **two mandatory tyre-change pit stops**, refuelling allowed.
- **Qualifying:** three-part knockout as introduced in 2006. That it was unchanged is assumed.
- **Points:** 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1. Lausitz paid half points (5-4-3-2.5-2-1.5-1-0.5).
- **Tyres:** Dunlop SP Sport Maxx, 265/660-R18 front and 280/660-R18 rear.

## Storylines
- **Hockenheim opener:** a first-lap pile-up. Kristensen spun after contact with Scheider, and in the smoke Prémat and Stoddart hit his car.
  - Prémat suffered a lumbar vertebra fracture and Kristensen whiplash.
  - Stand-ins: Biela (Oschersleben) and Winkelhock (Lausitz, Brands) for Kristensen; Werner for Prémat.
  - The race was restarted and finished on the time limit. Ekström won with an early-stop strategy.
- **Oschersleben:** the first win for a year-old car (Paffett, 2006 C-Klasse).
- **Lausitz safety-car fiasco:** the safety car picked up the wrong leader. The DMSB awarded half points, and race director Roland Bruynseraede was dismissed on 31 May.
- **Mugello:** Häkkinen won from 15th because he had made his second stop just before the safety car.
- **Zandvoort team orders row:** Prémat let Tomczyk through on the last-lap straight. Audi's Wolfgang Ullrich denied team orders.
- **Barcelona scandal** (see also the en.wiki and de.wiki texts):
  - Häkkinen took out title contender Tomczyk, and la Rosa took out Ekström.
  - After more Audi/Mercedes clashes, Audi withdrew all its remaining cars about 8–9 laps from the end. Ullrich said this was to stop "any driver doing something none of us want".
  - Only 6 Mercedes finished; Green won.
  - Häkkinen and la Rosa were disqualified, fined and given 10-place grid penalties for the finale.
  - The weights were reset from the lap-46 order.
- **Finale:** Ekström sealed the title at Hockenheim. Häkkinen retired from racing after the season.
- **Last year for Vodafone** as windscreen sponsor.
- **Team orders were banned from 2008** (de.wiki DTM), partly in response to this season's events.
