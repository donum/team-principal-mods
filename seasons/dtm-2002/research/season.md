# DTM 2002: research dossier

The third season of the revived DTM had 10 events with 2 races each (20 races). There were 6 events in Germany plus Zolder (BEL), Donington (GBR), the A1-Ring (AUT) and Zandvoort (NED). It ran from 21 Apr to 6 Oct, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
Laurent Aïello won the title in the Abt-Audi TT-R with 70 points, ahead of Bernd Schneider (64) and Mattias Ekström (50). It was the only title for the privately developed TT-R.

## Regulations (changes vs 2001)
- **Standing starts** replaced rolling starts [crash.net; de.wiki].
- **Points:**
  - Main race: **10-6-4-3-2-1** to the top 6 (the F1-style scale of the time).
  - Qualifying race: still **3-2-1**. This gave the sprint relatively more weight [crash.net].
- **HANS device** mandatory [en.wiki; totalmotorsport].
- **Bodywork:** 4-door saloons were allowed alongside 2-door coupés. A new double-profile rear wing was meant to equalise the different body shapes [crash.net; totalmotorsport].
- **Tyres:**
  - Dunlop slicks and wets: front 240/650R18 (previously 235/640R18), rear 280/650R18.
  - The new tyres gave more grip but wore faster, which made the mandatory stop more tactical [totalmotorsport, 30 May 2002].
- **Engines:**
  - 4.0 V8, rear-wheel drive, 90° bank angle, 102 mm cylinder spacing, chain-driven camshafts, minimum engine weight 165 kg.
  - Beryllium, titanium and ceramics banned.
  - Two sealed identical engines per car per season, used alternately [totalmotorsport].
- **Dimensions:** length 4.25–4.80 m. The base model needs at least 10,000 road cars built in one year [totalmotorsport].
- **Electronics:** brakes are the same for all teams; ABS and traction control banned. X-Trac and Hewland gearboxes approved [totalmotorsport].
- **Unchanged from 2001:**
  - Qualifying: 45 minutes, 12 laps.
  - QR of about 30–35 km.
  - Main race of about 100 km with 1 mandatory stop (4 tyres, no refuelling).
  - 1080 kg minimum weight (per car data; no 2002-specific source).

## Teams and people
- **Mercedes, HWA works cars:**
  - Vodafone (#1 Schneider, #2 Alesi)
  - Warsteiner (#5 Alzen, #6 Fässler)
- **Mercedes customer teams:**
  - Persson (Original-Teile)
  - Manthey (OASE #16, CEB #17)
  - Rosberg (Service 24h)
- **Abt:** 5 cars in three entries: Team Abt Sportsline (#3/#10), Team Abt (#4/#9) and the Junior team (#23 Tomczyk).
- **Opel:**
  - Phoenix: Reuter and Winkelhock.
  - Holzer: Scheider and Bartels.
  - Euroteam: Menu, plus a rotating second car driven by Olivier, then one-offs for JJ Lehto, Éric Hélary and Johnny Cecotto.
- **Team principals:** not documented in the sources checked.

## Storylines
- **Aïello's title.** Aïello won 8 races (QR and main combined) and beat Schneider by 6 points. It was the first DTM title for an Audi-based car since 1991, and a privateer one.
- **Alesi.** The ex-F1 driver won both races at Donington, the DTM's first UK round.
- **Mercedes still won the makes table.** Mercedes beat Audi 147–145 in de.wiki's Markenwertung, and HWA's Vodafone pair won the teams' classification.
- **Hockenheim rebuild.** Hockenheim was being rebuilt: the April round used a short course of about 2.6 km, and the October finale ran on the new 4.574 km GP layout.
- **Donington QR start crash:** Alzen hit the stalled Winkelhock. Both drivers were unhurt.
