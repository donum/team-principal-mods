# 2011 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 25th DTM season and the 12th since 2000. Ten championship rounds from 1 May (Hockenheim) to 23 Oct (Hockenheim).
- A non-championship show event was held in Munich's Olympic Stadium on 16–17 Jul. It used a Race of Champions-style knockout format and is listed as round "NC" in calendar.csv.
- A planned Shanghai round on 6 Nov was dropped on 1 Apr, because it clashed with the WTCC's Chinese round.
- Spielberg (Red Bull Ring, formerly A1-Ring) returned to the calendar and Adria was dropped.
- Last season of the four-door saloon silhouettes and of the A4 and W204 generation.
- 18 cars and 19 drivers. Kristensen stood in once.
- Champion: **Martin Tomczyk** (Audi Sport Team Phoenix, Audi A4 DTM 2008-spec), 72 pts. He won the title in a year-old car, ahead of Ekström (52) and Spengler (51).
- Manufacturer title: Audi, per de.wiki DTM.

## Regulations
- The development freeze continued, with 2008 and 2009 cars.
- 4.0 V8, about 460 hp. Weight 1,050 kg including driver (en.wiki car articles).
- **Tyres: Hankook** was the new single supplier.
  - One slick (Ventus F200) and one wet tyre (Ventus Z207).
  - Sizes 260/660-R18 front and 280/660-R18 rear (en.wiki 2012). en.wiki W204 gives the front as 265.
- **Qualifying:** knockout Q1–Q3 plus a single-lap Q4 shootout of the top 4. Segment details are not verified.
- **Race:** one race per event of about 160–190 km. Two mandatory pit stops, with refuelling still allowed (banned from 2012). There was a race time limit: Brands Hatch was stopped at 88 of 98 laps and Oschersleben at 45 of 50 when it ran out (de.wiki race reports). Norisring was stopped at 64 of 82 laps because of rain.
- **Points:** 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1 for the top 8. Team points were the sum of both cars.

## Cars and engines
Same as 2010: Audi A4 DTM 2008/2009 and AMG-Mercedes C-Klasse W204 2008/2009, both with 4.0 V8s of about 460 hp, at 1,050 kg. Mercedes switched to Petronas lubricants in 2011.

## Team principals / bosses
- Audi: Wolfgang Ullrich (not fetched).
- Mercedes: Norbert Haug.
- BMW: Jens Marquardt became BMW Motorsport Director, succeeding Mario Theissen on 1 Jul 2011. He was preparing the 2012 BMW entry.
- Teams as in 2010: Abt (Hans-Jürgen Abt), Phoenix (Ernst Moser), Rosberg, Persson (Ingmar Persson), Mücke (Peter Mücke) and HWA.

## Storylines
- Spengler (Mercedes) led after 5 rounds. Tomczyk took the lead with wins at Spielberg, Lausitz and Brands Hatch, and claimed the title at Valencia.
- Rockenfeller was hurt in a Le Mans crash. Tom Kristensen replaced him at Lausitz.
- Rookies: Mortara, Albuquerque, Vietoris, van der Zande and Frey.
- Paul di Resta left for F1, and Green moved into the HWA 2009 car.
- Last race for the four-door cars. BMW tested ahead of its 2012 return.
