# 2018 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- **10 weekends with 2 races each, so 20 races.** The season ran from 5 May to 14 October, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- 18 regular cars, 6 per make. Each make could also field one guest car for a single weekend, which did not score points.
- The **last season of the 4.0 V8 engines (used since 2000)**, and **Mercedes' final season**.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **Gary Paffett** (Mercedes-AMG Motorsport PETRONAS) with 255 points, 4 ahead of René Rast on 251. Paffett clinched it with 3rd in the last race. Rast won the final **six races in a row**, a series record.
  - Teams: Mercedes-AMG Motorsport PETRONAS
  - Manufacturers: Mercedes-Benz, its first makes' title since 2010

## Regulations
- **Format:** 55 min + 1 lap per race, the same as 2017, with one mandatory pit stop in each race. The stop could be taken at any time. Since 2017 the Saturday race also had a mandatory stop (de.wiki DTM-Saison 2017, Autosport).
- **Qualifying:** a separate session before each race. The top 3 scored 3-2-1 points.
- **Race points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- **Guest/wildcard entries:** new for 2018. They did not score, and drivers behind a guest moved up the points order.
- **Aero:** downforce was cut by about a third. The front had fewer and smaller flicks (one per side), a smaller splitter, simpler side ducts, and the end plates below the doors were removed. Wheel arches became standard parts. The third spring at the front axle was dropped (touringcartimes.com and Auto Bild, Feb 2018; motorsport.com, 1 Mar 2018). en.wiki's wording ("side winglets and fences enforced") is muddled.
- **Weight:** minimum weight fell to **1,115 kg** including driver, 10 kg less than the 1,125 kg of 2017. Without driver the minimum was 1,031 kg (touringcartimes.com, motorsport.com). No performance weights.
- **Engines:** 8 engines per car per season, up from 7, to cover the extra Brands Hatch weekend. Engine changes within this limit carried no penalty (touringcartimes.com, Auto Bild). en.wiki calls them "eight set engines" per driver.
- **Tyres:** Hankook only. One dry slick compound for every race, the tyre introduced in 2017. The option (soft) tyre used up to 2016 no longer existed. Tyre warmers were banned from 2017 (de.wiki DTM-Saison 2017, Autosport, Tyrepress). Late in the season a minimum tyre pressure of 1.3 bar was enforced (motorsport.com, Sept 2018).
- **DRS:** usable on 12 laps per race, 36 activations in total, only when within one second of the car ahead (motorsport.com and thecheckeredflag.co.uk, Jan 2019, describing the 2018 rule). In 2019 the window grew to three seconds.
- **Pit boards:** only to call a driver in or to warn of an emergency. Two mechanics could now carry tyres to the pit lane.
- **Misano:** raced at night.

## Cars and engines
- The cars and engines carried over from 2017: naturally aspirated 4.0 V8s with about 500 hp and 29 mm restrictors.
  - Audi RS 5 DTM
  - BMW M4 DTM (P66/1)
  - Mercedes-AMG C63 DTM (HWA)

## Storylines
- Mercedes' farewell season. It won the drivers' and manufacturers' titles.
- Rast's late surge almost overturned Paffett's lead.
- Calendar:
  - Returning: Brands Hatch (Grand Prix circuit, first visit since 2013 on the Indy layout).
  - New: Misano, first time in Italy since 2010, as a night race.
  - Dropped: Moscow.
  - The Lausitzring went back to the long Grand Prix course for the first time since 2004.
- Newcomers: Robin Frijns, Philipp Eng and Joel Eriksson. Returning: Pascal Wehrlein (from F1) and Daniel Juncadella.
- Leaving: Ekström (to World Rallycross), Wickens (IndyCar), Martin (WEC with Aston Martin), Blomqvist and Engel.
