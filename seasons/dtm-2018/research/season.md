# 2018 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- **10 weekends with 2 races each, so 20 races.** The season ran from 5 May to 14 October, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- 18 regular cars, 6 per make. Each make could also field one guest car for a single weekend, which did not score points.
- The **last season of the 4.0 V8 engines (used since 2000)**, and **Mercedes' final season**.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **Gary Paffett** (Mercedes-AMG Motorsport PETRONAS) with 255 points, 4 ahead of René Rast on 251. Paffett clinched it with 3rd in the last race. Rast won the final **six races in a row**, a series record.
  - Teams: Mercedes-AMG Motorsport PETRONAS
  - Manufacturers: Mercedes-Benz, its first makes' title since 2010

## Regulations
- **Format:** 55 min + 1 lap per race, the same as 2017, with a mandatory pit stop in each race.
- **Qualifying:** a separate session before each race. The top 3 scored 3-2-1 points.
- **Race points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- **Guest/wildcard entries:** new for 2018. They did not score, and drivers behind a guest moved up the points order.
- **Aero:** side winglets and fences were removed to reduce downforce.
- **Weight:** minimum weight fell to **1,115 kg** including driver. en.wiki gives 1,125 kg before this. No performance weights.
- **Engines:** 8 engine "sets" per driver per season, with components counted together. en.wiki says the previous rule was 7 complete engines.
- **Tyres:** Hankook.
- **Misano:** raced at night.

## Cars and engines
- The cars and engines carried over from 2017: naturally aspirated 4.0 V8s with about 500 hp and 29 mm restrictors.
  - Audi RS 5 DTM
  - BMW M4 DTM (P66/1)
  - Mercedes-AMG C63 DTM (HWA)

## Storylines
- Mercedes' farewell season. It won the drivers' and manufacturers' titles.
- Rast's late surge almost overturned Paffett's lead.
- Calendar:
  - Returning: Brands Hatch (Grand Prix circuit, first visit since 2013 on the Indy layout).
  - New: Misano, first time in Italy since 2010, as a night race.
  - Dropped: Moscow.
  - The Lausitzring went back to the long Grand Prix course for the first time since 2004.
- Newcomers: Robin Frijns, Philipp Eng and Joel Eriksson. Returning: Pascal Wehrlein (from F1) and Daniel Juncadella.
- Leaving: Ekström (to World Rallycross), Wickens (IndyCar), Martin (WEC with Aston Martin), Blomqvist and Engel.
