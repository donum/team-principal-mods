# Extra free agents (DTM 2018 pack)

A small pool of drivers outside the covered feeder series who could plausibly join a DTM team after March 2018. Data in `free_agents.csv`.

- `f1_starts` counts F1 starts before the 2018 season.
- `f1_years` lists F1 race seasons before 2018.
- `status_2018` is what the driver actually did in 2018.
- Groups: `ex_f1`, `ex_dtm` (left the DTM after 2017), `junior` (young drivers with a maker link).

## Suggested (8)

### Ex-F1

- **Robert Kubica.** Williams reserve and development driver in 2018, no race seat. Tested a Mercedes DTM car in 2013. Raced DTM in 2020 with Orlen Team ART (BMW).

### Ex-DTM

- **Maxime Martin.** BMW DTM driver 2014 to 2017 with 2 wins. Left BMW at the end of 2017 and joined Aston Martin in WEC.
- **Tom Blomqvist.** BMW DTM driver 2015 to 2017. In 2018 he raced Formula E for Andretti and a BMW M8 GTE in WEC, and won the Spa 24 Hours. Still a BMW works driver.
- **Maro Engel.** Mercedes DTM driver 2008 to 2011 and in 2017. In 2018 he raced Formula E for Venturi and won the Blancpain Endurance title with Black Falcon (Mercedes-AMG). Back in DTM from 2022 in the GT3 era.

### Juniors

- **Kelvin van der Linde.** Audi Sport GT3 driver in the ADAC GT Masters in 2018. Raced DTM for Abt (Audi) from 2021.
- **Sheldon van der Linde.** Audi GT3 driver in the ADAC GT Masters in 2018. Moved to BMW and the DTM (BMW Team RBM) for 2019.
- **Jake Dennis.** Red Bull F1 simulator driver and Aston Martin GT3 driver with R-Motorsport in 2018. Raced DTM in 2019 with R-Motorsport (Aston Martin).
- **Harrison Newey.** FIA F3 European in 2017, LMP2 in the European Le Mans Series in 2018. Raced DTM in 2020 with Audi Sport Team WRT.

## Not suggested, short reasons

- Giovinazzi: Sauber and Ferrari F1 reserve in 2018 and tied to Ferrari. He did 2 Audi DTM guest races in 2015.
- Palmer: no race programme in 2018, worked for BBC Radio 5 Live. No DTM link.
- Wickens: left Mercedes DTM for a full IndyCar seat with Schmidt Peterson.
- Ekström: retired from DTM in January 2018 for World RX. He is already in the 2018 DTM entry list as a farewell guest at round 1.

## Excluded

- In the 2018 DTM: Wehrlein (back from F1), Farfus (BMW until the end of 2018), Mortara, Frijns, Eng, Eriksson, and the guests Ogier, Zanardi and Ekström.
- Raced the covered FIA F3 European series in 2018: Schumacher, Ticktum, Aberdein, Habsburg, Scherer and the rest of that field.
- Maximilian Günther (Mercedes DTM junior) raced Formula 2 with Arden in 2018. Not researched further.

## Uncertain

- The covered feeder series for the 2018 pack were not known when this list was made. If the ADAC GT Masters becomes a covered series, drop both van der Lindes from this list.
- Jake Dennis birth date: en.wiki and the Formula E site give 16 June 1995. driverdb gives 30 June 1995. 16 June is used.
- Harrison Newey birth date: second source is the W Racing Team driver page as seen in a search result. The page itself was not opened.
- Blomqvist nationality: en.wiki now calls him British-born Swedish. He raced as British in 2018. The CSV uses british.
- Engel and Kelvin van der Linde later DTM seasons are in the GT3 era, with different cars.
