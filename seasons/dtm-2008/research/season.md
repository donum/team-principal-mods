# 2008 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 11 rounds, one race each, from Hockenheim (13 Apr) to Hockenheim (26 Oct). Le Mans Bugatti returned as an 11th event. The Düsseldorf presentation was on 6 Apr.
- **Champion:** Timo Scheider (Abt Audi A4 DTM 2008), 75 points and 3 wins. Scheider had never won a DTM race before this season; his first win came at Oschersleben in his 8th DTM year.
  - Runner-up: Paul di Resta, 71.
  - 3rd: Mattias Ekström, 56.
- **Teams' title:** Mercedes-Benz Bank AMG-Mercedes (Spengler/di Resta), 109 points.
- **Brand title:** Mercedes-Benz (de.wiki). Points were not found.
- Field: 19 cars. Mücke cut from 3 cars to 2.

## Regulations
- **Car generations:**
  - Audi introduced the new aero-focused A4 (R14).
  - Because of the 2007 C-Klasse model change, Mercedes ran no 2006 cars. The 2007 test chassis was allowed by exemption as a 5th Jahreswagen.
  - Audi still fielded two 2006 cars (Futurecom).
- **Placement weights** (de.wiki DTM):
  - Initially the 2006 Audis were grouped with the 2007 cars.
  - Within a group, the best-placed car gains **5 kg** at the next race, the second keeps its weight and the worst loses 5 kg.
  - Because the 2006 Audi always ended up third, the 2007 cars got progressively heavier, at times heavier than 2008 cars.
  - At mid-season, the base weights of all year-old cars were cut by **10 kg**, and the 2006 and 2007 Audis were scored together. touringcartimes (Jul 2008) gives the new base weights: 2006 cars 1,020 kg and 2007 cars 1,030 kg.
- **Pit stops:**
  - Two mandatory tyre-change stops.
  - First half of the season: both stops had to be made in the **middle third** of the race.
  - Second half: the window was widened to the **2nd and 3rd quarters** of the race (de.wiki; touringcartimes).
  - Refuelling was allowed.
- **Safety car:** reverted to the old rule, with the pit lane staying open.
- **Team orders banned** from 2008 (de.wiki).
- **Lausitz:** a new pit entry after Turn 3 removed the 2007 shortcut advantage.
- **Qualifying:**
  - de.wiki's Nürburgring report mentions Q1, Q2 and Q3, with Q1 times counting when later parts are cancelled.
  - en.wiki (2009 article) says 2008 had "two sessions". This is a conflict.
- **Points:** 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1.
- **Tyres:** Dunlop.

- de.wiki says the new A4 was immediately the fastest car, and Audi took a 1-2-3 at the Hockenheim opener.

## Storylines
- **Ralf Schumacher** debuted after 11 F1 seasons (Mücke, year-old car) and scored 3 points.
- **Häkkinen** retired. Paul di Resta was promoted to HWA and fought Scheider for the title to the finale.
- **Christijan Albers** returned from F1. Champ Car driver **Katherine Legge** joined Futurecom.
- **Mugello:** Scheider jumped the start from pole and fell out of the points.
- **Nürburgring:** rain stopped qualifying. Audi then fitted rain tyres to almost all cars on a dry track, and Mercedes ran 1–7 at one stage. It was Bernd Schneider's last win.
- **Catalunya:** Ekström was disqualified and Green got a 30 s penalty after a rough last-lap duel.
- **Retirement:** Bernd Schneider, the four-time new-DTM champion with five DTM titles in total, retired after the season.

## Teams and people
- **Mercedes:** HWA ran the works team; Persson (Ingmar Persson) and Mücke ran year-old cars.
- **Audi:** Abt (Hans-Jürgen Abt), Phoenix, Rosberg and Futurecom (Colin Kolles). Audi motorsport head was Wolfgang Ullrich.
