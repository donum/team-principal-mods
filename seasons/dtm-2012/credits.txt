DTM 2012 - Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters

Season research sources: de/en Wikipedia, racingyears.com, Wikidata.
Sponsors and livery colours were read from the car drawings below.
See seasons/dtm-2012/README.md in the project repository for details.

Car images (assets/Cars):
  Drawn by Pappydhorn12 and used with the artist's permission. All rights
  to these drawings stay with the artist; they are not covered by the
  pack's Creative Commons licences.

Team list icons (assets/logos):
  Each team is shown with its maker's emblem (Audi, BMW or Mercedes-Benz),
  as on the TV timing graphics. See the maker logos below.

Track maps (assets/tracks):
  Race-day illustrations made in the race-track-themes project, shared with
  the DTM 2013 pack (the eight venues both seasons raced on; the trackside
  branding is 2013's). The scenery is AI-rendered. The track surface,
  kerbs, pit lane and paddock layout are drawn over it from OpenStreetMap
  data (see the map data credit below). Valencia keeps the base map.
