# DTM 2012 photo candidates

Candidates for the main menu background, the per-team dashboard backgrounds
and five driver portraits. Nothing is downloaded into the repo and nobody was
contacted. Checked on 12 November 2026.

## Where the photos are

- **Wikimedia Commons.** The category tree under
  [2012 in Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:2012_in_Deutsche_Tourenwagen_Masters)
  and [2012 DTM season cars](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:2012_DTM_season_cars)
  holds 289 files, all CC BY or CC BY-SA. There are per-car categories such as
  "Audi A5 DTM (R17) of Mike Rockenfeller in 2012". Events covered: Hockenheim
  (April), Brands Hatch (May), the Norisring (July) and Zandvoort (August).
  Nothing from the Lausitzring, Spielberg, the Nürburgring, Oschersleben,
  Valencia or the Hockenheim final.
- **Most of it is Brands Hatch, 18 and 19 May 2012.** Three Flickr users shot
  the weekend and everything is on Commons already:
  - Rowan Harrison (CC BY-SA 2.0, up to 3872 x 2592): sharp shots at Paddock
    Hill Bend with the crowd and E-Postbrief banners. The best set.
  - Tom Bayly (tombayly13, CC BY-SA 2.0, 4288 x 2848): pit exit, pit lane
    and close-ups on Friday.
  - Micheal Evans (mdeuk, CC BY 2.0, only 1280 x 848): panning shots.
  - Ian McWilliams (supermac1961, CC BY 2.0, 2816 x 1880): a few cars plus
    Wolff, Coulthard and Schumacher off the car.
- **Smaller sets:** Cord Rodefeld (Hockenheim, CC BY-SA 2.0, up to
  5184 x 3456, more on his Flickr `rodefeld`), Kathrin Mezger (Hockenheim pit
  walk, CC BY-SA 2.0, cars in the garages), flughafen (Hockenheim and
  Norisring, CC BY 2.0, small), s.yuki (Norisring, CC BY 2.0, 4912 x 3264),
  André Zehetbauer (Norisring, CC BY-SA 2.0, 1280 px), Martin de Witte
  (Zandvoort, CC BY 2.0, 1200 px except one).
- **Mark Seymour and Kevin McGill:** no 2012 DTM photos found. Seymour's
  Flickr DTM search only finds 2013. McGill's Flickr account now shows 19
  photos and none are DTM; his 2013 photos survive on Commons only, and
  Commons has nothing from him for 2012.
- **Dennis de Wolde (InMotion Photos), permission granted for any DTM photo on
  his site.** His
  [Zandvoort 2012 album](https://inmotion-photos.com/motorsport/dtm-at-circuit-zandvoort-2012/)
  (26 August 2012, 29 photos, 900 px, the first four 1190 px, one 1920 px
  header) is saved locally in `reference/inmotion-photos/dtm-zandvoort-2012/`.
  All cars on track except two in the Abt Sportsline garage. Ten of the eleven
  teams appear (no Wolff, no Vietoris, no Hand).
- **Spieler Family (Jörg):** no 2012 DTM photos. All 69 hits for "DTM" in his
  Flickr photos are the 15 September 2013 Oschersleben batch (IDs 9873...),
  and no album is about motorsport.

Licence notes as for 2013: CC BY needs the credit; CC BY-SA edits must be
shared under CC BY-SA. All Commons links below are file pages, where the
original Flickr link and the licence are shown.
