# ADAC Formel Masters 2012 (Formel ADAC)

Fifth season of the ADAC's entry-level single-seater series. Lowest feeder series in the pack.

## Car
- One-make: Dallara monocoque ("Formel ADAC" car; Commons calls it Dallara Formulino), Volkswagen 1.6-litre FSI engine, 145 hp. Dunlop tyres (Dunlop logos visible on the 2012 Kirchhöfer car photo).
- Car price 49,900 euros, ordered from ADAC Motorsport in Munich. Source: the official regulations page; its text was written for 2012 (see 2013 dossier).
- Official team pages list the car as "ADAC Formel" for every 2012 team.

## Format
- 8 race weekends, 3 races each, 24 races planned. Practice and qualifying Friday (qualifying Saturday morning at Sachsenring), race 1 Saturday, races 2 and 3 Sunday. At Nürburgring I races 1 and 2 were both on Saturday (official portrait timetable and de Wikipedia).
- 23 races were run. Sachsenring race 3 was abandoned after Florian Herzog and Kim-Alexander Giersiepen hit a course car while marshals recovered Nicolas Pohler's car. No points were given for it (en and de Wikipedia).
- Seven weekends were on the ADAC Masters Weekend bill with ADAC GT Masters. The first Nürburgring weekend (13-15 July) supported the ADAC Truck Grand Prix instead (en and de Wikipedia).
- The official site names the two Nürburgring events "Nürburgring I" and "Nürburgring II".
- Points, races 1 and 2: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- Points, race 3: 15-10-8-7-6-5-4-3-2-1.
- No bonus points for pole or fastest lap (same scale as 2013; Wikipedia lists none).
- Team championship also run. Official totals: Lotus 651, Neuhauser Racing 494, Mücke Motorsport 470, G&J / Schiller Motorsport 258, HS Engineering 94, Team KUG Motorsport 53, KSW Motorsport 13. These are not sums of the drivers' points (KSW has 13 while Giersiepen scored 0), so team points use their own method. Method not found.
- ADAC Berlin-Brandenburg e.V. (Grapp) is not listed on its own in the team standings. It is probably counted with Mücke Motorsport. Not confirmed.

## Grid
- 20 drivers started at least one race. 8 entrants on the official entry list (7 in the team standings).
- Cars per weekend (counted from the entry list): 18 at round 1, 17 at round 2, 17 at round 3, 18 at round 4, 17 at round 5, 16 at round 6, 16 at round 7, 17 at round 8.
- Hubertus-Carlos Vier drove for two entrants: Team KUG Motorsport (car 16) at round 1, then Lotus (car 4) at rounds 3 and 4. His official portrait (July 2012) lists him with Lotus.
- Beitske Visser missed rounds 3 and 7. Kuba Dalewski left after round 5. Antti Buri drove an extra Lotus at round 7 only.
- Felix Wieland was the youngest driver (15 at debut). Antti Buri was the oldest (23) (de Wikipedia; matches official birth dates).

## Age rules
- "Youngsters from the age of 15 upwards" (official regulations page, archived 2013, text written for 2012). Exact cut-off date not found.

## Champion
- Marvin Kirchhöfer (GER, Lotus), 329 points, 9 wins from 23 races. Rookie season. Checked on the archived official standings page (October 2012), en Wikipedia and de Wikipedia. All three agree.
- Gustav Malja second (307), Jeffrey Schmidt third (193).
- Kirchhöfer won all three races in the Hockenheim final.
- Winners: Kirchhöfer 9, Malja 3, Picariello 2, Visser 2, Grapp 2, and Jäger, Dalewski, Kremer, Nissany, Schmidt 1 each (23 races).

## DTM links
- No 2012 Formel Masters driver raced in DTM in 2012 or 2013 (checked against seasons/dtm-2012/research/entries.csv and seasons/dtm-2013/research/entries.csv).
- No 2012 driver found with a later DTM start. Checked en Wikipedia articles for Kirchhöfer, Malja, Schmidt, Jäger, Picariello, Dontje, Visser, Nissany, Beer and Balthasar. Not checked for Caspari, Herzog, Pohler, Wieland, Vier, Giersiepen, Grapp, Kremer, Dalewski, Buri.

## Later careers (as of 2026 sources)
- Marvin Kirchhöfer: German F3 champion 2013, GP3 race winner, one GP2 season, then GT racing; McLaren factory driver.
- Gustav Malja: Formula Renault, then Formula 2.
- Roy Nissany: Formula 2 (up to 2023), then sportscars.
- Alessio Picariello: 2013 Formel Masters champion with Mücke, later GT World Challenge Europe Endurance champion (2025).
- Beitske Visser: GP3, Formula Renault 3.5, W Series.
- Others (Schmidt, Jäger, Dontje, Beer) went to F3 and GT racing; details not checked in depth.

## Repeat drivers in 2013 Formel Masters
- Kremer, Picariello, Dontje, Visser, Grapp, Giersiepen, Herzog, Beer. Birth dates match the 2013 file.
- Florian Herzog: the 2013 file left his birth date empty. The official 2012 portrait gives 17.09.1993, same as driverdb. Used here.

## Data notes and uncertainties
- Driver points: official ADAC standings (archived 29 Oct 2012) match de Wikipedia exactly. en Wikipedia has Beer on 13 instead of 17. The en article on Beer says he was a guest and could not score. The official table gives him 17 points and position 16, so the official value is used.
- Team points: en Wikipedia has a different team table (Lotus 461, Mücke 366, Neuhauser 355, G&J/Schiller 215, HS 63). Official values used.
- Team name: the official site writes "G&J / Schiller Motorsport". Wikipedia writes "G&J/Schiler" (typo).
- Grapp's entrant on the official entry list is "ADAC Berlin-Brandenburg e.V.", separate from Mücke Motorsport. de Wikipedia links it to Mücke. Kept as its own entrant here.
- Team bases are taken from the 2013 file for the same teams. The G&J / Schiller base (Siegburg) is a guess from 2013 Schiller Motorsport and is not confirmed for 2012.
- Rookie flags: based on the official 2011 driver portrait list (archived), news quoted in en Wikipedia and driver articles. Thomas Jäger did one 2011 round (Assen), so he is marked as not a full rookie.
- Birth dates: all 20 from the official 2012 driver portraits. Cross-checked with en Wikipedia for Kirchhöfer, Malja, Schmidt, Jäger, Picariello, Dontje, Visser, Nissany, Beer and Balthasar (all match). Single-source (official portrait only): Dalewski, Vier, Buri, Herzog, Caspari, Kremer, Grapp, Pohler, Giersiepen, Wieland. Kremer, Grapp and Giersiepen also match the 2013 file, which used the 2013 portraits.
- Sachsenring is listed with 3 races in the calendar file because 3 were planned. Only 2 were scored.
- Team colours: only Lotus is photo-confirmed (black and gold). The other teams are left empty. The 2012 group photo on Commons shows overalls, not cars.
- Pole positions and fastest laps were not collected.
