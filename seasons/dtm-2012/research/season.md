# 2012 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 26th DTM season and the 13th since 2000. Ten championship rounds from 29 Apr to 21 Oct, opening and closing at Hockenheim. A non-championship Munich stadium show was held on 14–15 Jul.
- **New regulations.** BMW returned with works cars for the first time since its 1992 DTM exit (en.wiki says "since 1994"), with 6 cars from Schnitzer, RBM and RMG. Audi (A5 DTM, 8 cars) and Mercedes (C-Coupé, 8 cars) completed a 22-car field.
- Champion: **Bruno Spengler** (BMW Team Schnitzer, BMW M3 DTM), 149 pts, ahead of Paffett (145) and Green (121).
- The first official manufacturer standings were won by BMW (346) from Audi (335) and Mercedes (329). Teams' title: BMW Team Schnitzer.

## Regulations (new for 2012)
- **Body:** two-door coupé silhouettes with a common CFRP monocoque, safety cell and integrated seat. Many common parts were meant to cut costs by up to 40%.
  - All cars used the same large single-element rear wing. Through-flow bodywork and extra rear wings were banned.
  - Dimensions: wheelbase 2,750 mm, width 1,950 mm, length 5,010 mm including the wing (4,650 mm without). Height 1.20 m for Mercedes and BMW, 1.15 m for Audi.
- **Weight:** 1,100 kg including driver (en.wiki and the car articles). de.wiki says 1,050 kg, which conflicts.
- **Engine:** 4.0 V8, naturally aspirated, maximum torque 500 Nm.
  - Quoted outputs: Mercedes 500 PS, BMW 480 PS, Audi 460 PS (de.wiki).
  - en.wiki car articles: BMW P66 at 480 hp/7,500 rpm, Mercedes about 460 hp.
- **Gearbox:** sequential 6-speed transaxle, now with paddle shift (Hewland).
- **Fuel:** refuelling banned, 120-litre tanks.
- **Tyres:** Hankook, larger sizes. Front 300/680-R18; rear 310/710 per de.wiki, or 320/700 per en.wiki (conflict).
- Flags were now also shown on the steering wheel ("Marshalling System").
- **Qualifying:** knockout Q1–Q3 plus a Q4 single-lap shootout of the top 4. Elimination numbers are not verified.
- **Race:** one race per event, two mandatory tyre-change stops inside pit windows. Per de.wiki 2013, the window rule was dropped in 2013.
- **Points:** new F1-style scale for the top 10: 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.

## Storylines
- BMW's return paid off: 4 Spengler wins and 1 for Farfus. Spengler beat Paffett by 4 pts at the Hockenheim finale.
- Mercedes won only 3 races (Paffett ×2, Green ×1).
- Mortara won twice for Audi, at Spielberg and Zandvoort.
- Tomczyk joined BMW as reigning champion. Spengler left Mercedes for BMW Schnitzer.
- Last DTM seasons for Coulthard, Ralf Schumacher, Susie Wolff and Rahel Frey.
