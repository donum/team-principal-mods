# 1986 Deutsche Tourenwagen-Meisterschaft (DTM)

Third season and the first under the DTM name (formerly DPM).

## Series & regulations
- ONS-sanctioned Group A touring cars with displacement-based weight and tyre handicaps ("Group A with weight and tire penalties" per TCR). Turbo cars were penalised heavily after 1985: Volvo 240 Turbos ran at 1400 kg (TCR Hockenheim report) and the Sierra XR4Ti also had to weigh 1400 kg by the AVUS round (TCR AVUS report).
- Scrutineering was enforced. Andersson was excluded at the AVUS (14 kg underweight) and Oberndorfer at Zolder II (exhaust manifold 0.3 mm over the limit).
- Points: 20-18-16-15-...-1 for the top 18 classified. 1 drop result (best 8 of 9). All races single-heat, full points.
- No documented mid-season rule changes.

## Weekend format
- Timed practice sets the grid; no qualifying race. One race of about 100-114 km per event, no mandatory pit stops.
- Zolder I was red-flagged after 11 laps and restarted, with the result on aggregate.
- Venues: Zolder (x2, the second on a resurfaced track), Hockenheim, Nürburgring (x3), AVUS, and the airfields Mainz-Finthen and Wunstorf.

## Storylines
- Kurt Thiim, the 1984 German F3 champion, was in only his second touring-car outing at Zolder. Driving Nickel's privateer ATN Rover (Austin Rover Germany support gone), he won the opener and the title with 130 points and 3 wins.
- Volker Weidler stood in for Oberndorfer at Marko RSM's AMG Mercedes from round 3 and won twice (Nürburgring, AVUS), finishing 2nd on 113 despite missing the first two rounds. The title was decided at Zolder II and the finale.
- Kurt König (privateer BMW 635) won Zolder II after Oberndorfer's disqualification and finished 3rd. Ford won twice (Niedzwiedz at Mainz, Reuter at the finale). Volvo won once (Andersson, Hockenheim).
- Niedzwiedz (HWRT Sierra) took 4 poles but retired often. Beate Nodes was 3rd at the AVUS, the first DTM podium by a woman.
- Field was smaller than 1985 but stronger at the front.

## Team principals / managers
Not documented in the sources used. Marko RSM ran the AMG-built Mercedes (TCR: "AMG Marko team"). ATN Autotechnik Nickel ran the Rover.

- DTM-specific weights per make are known only for Volvo and Sierra (1400 kg). Power/weight figures in the table are Group A (ETCC) values.
- Nine one-off entries at the Eifelrennen/Mainz/finale have no car number.
- team_base is mostly empty. Birth dates exist only where Wikidata has them.
- Tyres per entry are filled only for ATN (Dunlop), Marko RSM (Pirelli), IPS and Beckers Volvos (Goodyear) and Ford Ringshausen (Dunlop, from Reuter's finale; team-wide use assumed).
