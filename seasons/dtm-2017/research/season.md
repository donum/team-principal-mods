# 2017 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 9 weekends with 2 races each, so 18 races. The season ran from 6 May to 15 October, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- The field was cut from 24 to **18 cars**, 6 per make, to save costs.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **René Rast** (Audi Sport Team Rosberg) with 179 points, in his first full season. Ekström had 176, Green 173 and Rockenfeller 167.
  - Teams: Audi Sport Team Rosberg
  - Manufacturers: Audi

## Regulations
- **Format:** both races were 55 min + 1 lap, with **a mandatory pit stop in each race**. Previously Saturday was 40 min with no stop and Sunday was 60 min.
  - Pit crews were limited to **8 mechanics**, down from 15.
  - Only one wheel gun per side. en.wiki describes the result as wheels changed one after another, which made stops last up to about 10 s.
  - Tyre warmers were banned.
- **Qualifying:** a separate session before each race. The top 3 in qualifying scored **3-2-1 points**, new for 2017.
- **Race points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- **Team radio:** banned from team to driver (en.wiki, announced 1 May 2017).
- **Engines:**
  - Air restrictors grew from 28 mm to **29 mm**, and development of the intake system was allowed.
  - Power rose from about 460 hp to **about 500 hp**.
  - BMW's engine became the P66/1.
- **Aero:**
  - The **two-element rear wing** returned.
  - With DRS, only the upper wing element now flapped.
  - Ride height was raised and the front splitter, underbody and diffuser rules changed, all to reduce downforce.
- **Wheels and tyres:** ATS became the standard rim supplier. Hankook remained the tyre supplier and extended its contract to 2019, with new compounds for 2017.
- **Weights:** base weight was 1,125 kg, and performance weights ran within 1,095–1,140 kg. After protests from teams, **performance weights were abolished from round 8** (Red Bull Ring) onward, and all cars ran at the base weight.

## Cars and engines
- Audi RS 5 DTM: Audi 4.0 V8.
- BMW M4 DTM: P66/1 4.0 V8, over 500 hp at 7,500 rpm, 500 Nm.
- Mercedes-AMG C63 DTM: Mercedes-AMG/HWA 4.0 V8, over 500 hp.
- All naturally aspirated 90° V8s with Hewland gearboxes.

## Teams and people
- **Mercedes** dropped ART and Mücke, and HWA ran all 6 cars in three entries:
  - Mercedes me
  - SILBERPFEIL Energy
  - BWT
  - Ulrich Fritz was HWA team principal and Mercedes DTM boss.
- **Audi:** Abt Sportsline went from 4 cars to 2, alongside Rosberg (2) and Phoenix (2). Dieter Gass became Head of Audi Motorsport on 1 January 2017, succeeding Ullrich.
- **BMW:** MTEK and Schnitzer left. RBM and RMG ran 3 cars each.
  - For the teams' standings, the cars were split into RBM (#7, #36), RMG (#11, #15) and "BMW Team RMR" (#16 Glock, #31 Blomqvist).

## Storylines
- Mortara switched from Audi to Mercedes.
- Newcomers: Loïc Duval (Audi). Maro Engel returned to Mercedes.
- Leaving:
  - Tomczyk and Scheider retired.
  - Da Costa went to Formula E.
  - Molina and Tambay left.
  - Juncadella, Vietoris, Götz and Rosenqvist were dropped by Mercedes.
- **Zandvoort race 2:** Wittmann was disqualified for not having enough fuel, and the win went to Rockenfeller.
- **Mercedes exit:** announced on 25 July 2017. Mercedes would leave the DTM after 2018 for Formula E (2019/20).
