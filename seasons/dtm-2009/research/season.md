# 2009 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 10 rounds, one race each, from Hockenheim (17 May) to Hockenheim (25 Oct).
- The opener was moved 3 weeks later to avoid clashing with the Bahrain GP, and Lausitz moved to 31 May. Brands Hatch was moved a week later (Belgian GP clash).
- **Dijon-Prenois** debuted, replacing Le Mans. Mugello was dropped.
- The opener was the 100th race of the DTM since 2000.
- **Champion:** Timo Scheider (Abt Audi A4 DTM 2009), 64 points and 2 wins. He was the second driver after Bernd Schneider to defend a title in the new DTM.
  - Runner-up: Gary Paffett, 59 points and 4 wins.
  - 3rd: Paul di Resta, 45.
- **Teams' title:** Salzgitter/Mercedes-Benz Bank AMG-Mercedes (Paffett/Spengler), 100 points.
- **Brand title:** Mercedes-Benz (de.wiki). Points were not found.
- Field: 20 cars, 12 Audi and 8 Mercedes.
  - Audi: Abt 4 + Abt Lady Power 1 + Rosberg 2 + Phoenix 2 + Kolles/Futurecom 3.
  - Mercedes: HWA 4 + Persson 2 + Mücke 2.

## Regulations
Rule changes announced 21 Apr 2009 (crash.net; en.wiki).
- **Qualifying:** four-part knockout.
  - Q1: 14 min, top 14 advance.
  - Q2: 10 min. The number advancing is not stated; presumably 8.
  - Q3: 9 min, top 4 advance.
  - Q4: a single timed lap each, deciding rows 1–2.
  - Parc fermé between Q3 and Q4 (no refuelling, no tyre changes).
  - The weekend roll-out moved to Friday afternoon, with qualifying on Saturday afternoon.
- **Minimum weights:** 2009 cars 1,050 kg, 2008 cars 1,030 kg, 2007 cars 1,010 kg.
- **Success ballast continued:**
  - Audi said its 2009 A4 started with +20 kg over the 2008-spec cars.
  - Mercedes' 2009 cars carried +20 kg after their strong start (touringcartimes, Aug 2009).
  - Result-based handicapping was abolished for 2010.
- **Race:** two mandatory tyre-change stops, with refuelling allowed (the 70 L tank remained until 2011). Distances grew to about 174–198 km.
- **Team orders:** banned since 2008.
- **Points:** 10-8-6-5-4-3-2-1.
- **Tyres:** Dunlop SP Sport Maxx.

## Storylines
- **Scheider vs Paffett:**
  - Paffett led for much of the year.
  - Scheider was disqualified at Zandvoort, along with the Audis of Winkelhock, Prémat and Bakkerud; the reason was not found.
  - Scheider then finished 1st or 2nd at almost every remaining round, winning at Oschersleben and Catalunya.
  - Paffett won the last two races, but too late.
- **Kristensen** won the opener, his last touring-car win.
- **Year-old car wins:** Jamie Green won the Norisring again (year-old car).
- **Tomczyk** won the Nürburgring.
- **di Resta** won Brands Hatch and finished 3rd overall.
- **Ralf Schumacher** was promoted to an HWA new car and finished 11th with 9 points.
- **Retirements and departures:** Bernd Schneider retired; Albers left for Kolles' Le Mans programme.
- **Rookies:** Christian Bakkerud and Johannes Seidlitz (Kolles Futurecom), and Tomáš Kostka (Futurecom BRT).
- **Katherine Legge** moved to a dedicated Abt "Lady Power" team with a 2008 car.
