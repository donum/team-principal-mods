# 2013 Formula One World Championship: research dossier

## Overview
- 64th F1 World Championship. 19 rounds from 17 March (Melbourne) to 24 November (Interlagos). The last season of the 2.4 V8 engines.
- HRT left, so the grid had 11 teams and 22 cars. The Valencia (European) Grand Prix was dropped. The German Grand Prix was at the Nürburgring.
- Champion: **Sebastian Vettel** (Infiniti Red Bull Racing, Red Bull RB9), 397 pts, 13 wins, fourth title in a row. He clinched it in India (round 16) with three races left.
- Vettel won the last nine races in a row (Belgium to Brazil), a record for one season (en.wiki). 13 wins in a season equalled Michael Schumacher's record.
- Constructors: Red Bull Racing 596, Mercedes 360, Ferrari 354, Lotus 315, McLaren 122.
- Lewis Hamilton moved from McLaren to Mercedes, Sergio Pérez to McLaren. Mark Webber retired from F1 after the season.
- Five different winners: Vettel 13, Alonso 2, Rosberg 2, Räikkönen 1, Hamilton 1.

## Regulations
### Points
- 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the top 10. Classified at 90% of the winner's laps.

### Qualifying
- With 22 cars, Q1 (20 min) and Q2 (15 min) each eliminate the slowest 6 cars. Q3 (10 min), top 10. 107% rule as before.
- The top 10 start on the tyres of their fastest Q3 lap.

### Tyres (Pirelli)
- Dry compounds: hard (orange band from 2013), medium (white), soft (yellow), supersoft (red). Intermediate (green) and wet (blue).
- Two dry compounds per race, both must be used in a dry race.
- Tyre story (en.wiki season article and race articles):
  - Early in the season the tyres wore quickly; critics said tyre management mattered too much. In China the soft lasted about seven laps, so Pirelli changed the Bahrain option from soft to medium.
  - Pirelli changed the rear tyres for Canada to reduce delaminations (back to the 2011-2012 belt).
  - Mercedes ran a tyre test for Pirelli with its 2013 car and race drivers (timing after the Spanish Grand Prix is general knowledge). Ferrari and Red Bull protested and the FIA sent the case to its International Tribunal.
  - At Silverstone six tyres failed in the race, four of them explosive punctures. For the German Grand Prix Pirelli replaced the steel belt in the rear tyres with Kevlar. A new tyre followed after that (the en.wiki British GP article names the race as a missing link; the Hungarian Grand Prix is the usual date given, not checked).
- Minimum weight raised to 642 kg for the heavier 2013 tyres.

### DRS
- From 2013 DRS could be used only in the race zones during practice and qualifying too (en.wiki 2013 Australian GP article). Before, use was free in practice and qualifying.
- In the race: within one second at the detection point, not in the first two laps.

### Other rules
- KERS 60 kW, 400 kJ per lap (about 6.67 s per lap). No refuelling.
- 2.4 V8, 8 engines per driver. Gearbox for 5 events.
- Teams could cover the stepped nose with an optional "vanity panel".
- In-season testing ban. Pirelli's tyre tests with a current car were the subject of the Mercedes case above.

## Story of the season
- **Open first half.** Räikkönen won Australia for Lotus. Vettel won Malaysia by passing Webber late in the race against team orders (en.wiki). Alonso won China and Spain (Spain with four pit stops). Mercedes started from grid 1 in 7 of the first 10 races but lost places in the races through tyre wear; Rosberg won Monaco and Britain, Hamilton won Hungary.
- **Tyre change.** After the Silverstone failures Pirelli brought Kevlar-belted rear tyres from Germany. From the summer break on, Red Bull won every race.
- **Vettel's nine in a row.** Belgium, Italy, Singapore, Korea, Japan, India, Abu Dhabi, United States, Brazil. He took the title in India.
- **Lotus** was strong on tyre wear in the first half and often on the podium (Räikkönen, then Grosjean in the second half). Räikkönen missed the last two races.
- **McLaren** had no podium all year (best: Button 4th in Brazil).
- **Force India** scored 59 points in rounds 1-10 and 18 in rounds 11-19. Sauber did the opposite: 7 and 50 (Hülkenberg 4th in Korea).
