# 2014 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 28th DTM season and the 15th since 2000. Ten rounds from 4 May to 19 Oct, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- New venue: the Hungaroring, replacing Brands Hatch.
- A Guangzhou street race planned for 28 Sep was cancelled in July. Track work proved too extensive, talks over Zhuhai as a substitute failed, and Zandvoort replaced it.
- 23 cars: BMW 8, Audi 8, Mercedes 7. Mercedes ran a single-car HWA entry for Wehrlein (Gooix).
- BMW introduced the **M4 DTM**.
- Champion: **Marco Wittmann** (BMW Team RMG), 156 pts, with 4 wins. He clinched the title at Lausitz in round 8 and won by 50 pts from Ekström (106), with Rockenfeller 3rd (72).
- Manufacturers: Audi 411, BMW 380, Mercedes 219. Teams' title: BMW Team RMG (203).
- Last season with one race per weekend. From 2015 there were two races per weekend.

## Regulations (changes for 2014)
- **Qualifying:** F1-style three-part knockout (Q1/Q2/Q3). The top-4 single-lap shootout was dropped because ARD cut its TV airtime. Segment lengths and elimination numbers are not verified.
- **Pit stops:** reduced to one mandatory stop. It had to fall inside a pit window covering the middle third of the race.
  - Not allowed under a safety car. Allowed within 6 laps after a safety car even if outside the window.
  - The window was dropped for wet races after round 3.
- **Option (soft) tyre:** at most half the race distance could be run on it. From round 3 the limit was tightened: race half minus 1 lap for an even lap count, minus 1.5 laps for an odd one.
- **DRS:** now usable until the end of the race (in 2013 it was disabled for the last 3 laps). Also allowed at Zandvoort.
- **Performance weights:** each make carried weight based on results, applied to all its cars. Per de.wiki the maximum was 10 kg; the scale and steps are not verified.
- **Engines:** two spare engines per manufacturer.
- **Practice:** Friday rollout split into two 15-minute segments for odd and even car numbers. After round 3, Saturday's 90-minute practice became 2 × 60 minutes.
- **Weight:** 1,110 kg including driver (en.wiki).
- **Points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the top 10.

## Cars and engines
- **BMW M4 DTM:**
  - CFRP monocoque; L 4,775–5,010 mm, W 1,950 mm, WB 2,750 mm.
  - BMW P66 V8, 460 hp per en.wiki, 500 Nm.
  - Hewland gearbox, 1,110 kg, Castrol lubricants, BBS wheels.
- **Audi RS 5 DTM (2014):** 4.0 V8. Specs not fetched.
- **DTM AMG Mercedes C-Coupé (2014):** Mercedes-AMG 4.0 V8, about 460 hp, 1,110 kg.

## Storylines
- Wittmann, in his second season, took wins at Hockenheim, the Hungaroring, Spielberg and the Nürburgring. At 24 he was the youngest German DTM champion (BMW press).
- Audi went winless until Ekström won the last two races (Zandvoort and Hockenheim). Rockenfeller scored three 2nds but no wins.
- Other first-time or one-off winners:
  - Vietoris (Oschersleben)
  - Wickens (wet Norisring)
  - Maxime Martin (Moscow, rookie)
  - Wehrlein (Lausitz, first win at 19)
- Paul di Resta returned from F1. Vitaly Petrov joined Mercedes (Mücke) and scored 0 pts.
- Rookies: da Costa, Nico Müller and Maxime Martin.
