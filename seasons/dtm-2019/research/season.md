# 2019 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 9 weekends with 2 races each, so 18 championship races. The season ran from 4 May to 6 October, opening and closing at Hockenheim.
- There was also a non-championship **Super GT x DTM Dream Race** at Fuji on 23–24 November.
- The first season under **Class One** rules (shared with Super GT GT500): **2.0 L 4-cylinder turbo** engines replaced the V8s.
- Makes: Audi, BMW and **Aston Martin** (R-Motorsport, replacing Mercedes). It was the first non-German make since Alfa Romeo in 1996.
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **René Rast** (Audi Sport Team Rosberg) with 322 points. His second title, clinched at the Nürburgring, round 8.
  - Teams: Audi Sport Team Rosberg
  - Manufacturers: Audi, with four races to spare
- The 500th DTM race was Lausitz race 2.

## Regulations
- **Engine:** Class One 2.0 L inline-4, single turbo with a Garrett TR35R turbocharger.
  - About 610 hp and 9,500 rpm limit.
  - At least 6,000 km per engine. A change during a weekend meant a 10-place grid penalty.
  - Fuel: Aral Ultimate 102 RON.
- **Push-to-pass:** about **+30 hp, up to 12 times per race**, under the same conditions as DRS (en/de.wiki).
  - An Audi rules page summarised in search results gives a different limit: 60 seconds per race, from lap 2.
- **DRS:** allowed when within **3 s** of the car ahead, previously 1 s. In the last 5 laps, every driver except the leader could use it freely (de.wiki).
- **Weight:** 1,070 kg with driver and fuel, down from 1,115 kg. de.wiki gives 986 kg without driver and fuel, down from 1,031 kg.
- **Aero:** Super GT-based single-element rear wing, diffusers and underbody.
- **Safety and electronics:**
  - No launch control or start handbrake.
  - The interior mirror was replaced by a rear-view camera.
  - A firewall was added behind the driver.
  - Bosch electronics: DDU 10, MS 7.4 and PBX 190, plus a tyre pressure monitoring system.
- **Race length:**
  - Round 1 used **fixed lap counts** aimed at about 58 minutes, with a 70-minute cap and up to 3 safety-car laps added.
  - From round 2, races went back to **55 min + 1 lap**, and up to 2 safety-car laps could be added.
  - All races started at 13:30 local time.
- **Pit stops:**
  - 1 mandatory stop per race. It was not allowed under a safety car.
  - Crews were limited to 9 mechanics, per a search summary of Audi's rules page.
- **Tyres:** Hankook.
  - 7 sets of slicks per car per event, with at most 2 new sets in qualifying.
  - A separate limit for rain tyres.
  - These figures come from search summaries.
- **Qualifying:** a separate session before each race. The top 3 scored 3-2-1 points.
- **Race points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1.
- **Manufacturer points:** only the best 4 cars of each make counted per race, because the makes ran different numbers of cars.
- **Practice:** 2 sessions on Friday (45 and 30 min), down from 3.
- **Super GT guests:** a balance of performance applied to the Super GT cars, which were not yet Class One.

- **Audi:**
  - Rosberg: Rast, Green
  - Abt Sportsline: Frijns, Müller

  - R-Motorsport I: di Resta, Dennis

## Storylines
- Mercedes left. HWA moved to Formula E, and so did Paffett, Wehrlein and Mortara. Auer went to Super Formula.
- Aston Martin's debut season brought only modest results: 49 manufacturer points.
- Andrea Dovizioso (MotoGP) raced WRT's car at Misano. Fittipaldi moved to Rosberg that weekend to stand in for Jamie Green, who had appendicitis.
- **Hockenheim finale:** Honda (Jenson Button), Lexus (Hirakawa/Cassidy) and Nissan (Matsuda/Quintarelli) ran as guests.
- **Fuji Dream Race:** 34 and 31 laps. Winners: Nick Cassidy (Lexus) and Narain Karthikeyan (Honda). R-Motorsport did not enter.
- Calendar changes:
  - Dropped: Zandvoort, the Red Bull Ring and the Hungaroring.
  - Added: Assen.
  - Returning: Zolder, for the first time since 2002.
- Rast won 7 races.
