# 2024 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- **Season:** 38th DTM season, the 4th under GT3 rules and the 2nd organised by the ADAC.
- **Calendar:** 8 weekends, 16 races, from 27 April (Oschersleben) to 20 October (Hockenheim).
- **Drivers' champion:** **Mirko Bortolotti** (SSR Performance, Lamborghini Huracán GT3 Evo 2) with 238 points. Kelvin van der Linde (Abt, Audi) was second with 221 and Maro Engel (Winward, Mercedes) third with 203. This was Lamborghini's first DTM title.
- **Teams' title:** Schubert Motorsport (361).
- **Manufacturers' title:** Mercedes-AMG (434).

## Regulations
Changes from 2023 come from de.wiki and motorsport.com (2024). The base rules are the 2023 regulations.
- **Weekend:** FP1 lengthened from 45 to 55 min; FP2 stays at 45 min. The two 20-min qualifying sessions are unchanged.
- **Races:** 60 min + 1 lap. The 2025 regulations shortened this "from 60 min + 1 lap" (en.wiki 2025).
- **Mandatory stop:**
  - A tyre change under the safety car or a full course yellow (FCY) no longer counts as the mandatory stop.
  - Pit window: assumed unchanged from 2023 at minutes 20–40. Not verified.
  - Race director Sven Stoppe aimed to avoid using the safety car during the pit window.
- **Tyres:** The **top 10 qualifiers** (previously top 5) must start the race on the tyres they qualified on. The supplier is Pirelli.
- **Testing restrictions** (from 1 March 2024), per driver:
  - All tests must be registered.
  - Testing allowed at a maximum of 5 tracks.
  - Maximum of 5 test days.
  - Maximum of 3 tyre sets.
  - No testing at the race track in race week.
  - Thursday tests dropped.
- **Three-car teams:** Schubert could apply for a second pit station.
- **Unchanged from 2023 (assumed, 2024 regulations PDF not found):**
  - Success ballast of 20/10/5 kg.
  - BoP rules.
  - Points: top 15 scoring 25…1, plus 3-2-1 for qualifying.
  - Two best cars per team/make count.

## Storylines
- Bortolotti and SSR won the title in the finale against Kelvin van der Linde.
- Abt's last season with Audi. Audi's customer-racing withdrawal left Abt to switch to Lamborghini for 2025.
- Aitken (Emil Frey Ferrari) won 3 races: Oschersleben R1, Zandvoort R1 and Sachsenring R1.
- Engstler won Oschersleben R2 and Hockenheim R2.
- The Lausitzring moved from August to May. The rest of the calendar order was unchanged.
