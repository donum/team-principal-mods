# DTM 2000: research dossier

The first season of the revived DTM (Deutsche Tourenwagen Masters). It had 9 events and 18 planned races, all in Germany. Lausitz (rd 6) was cancelled because of heavy rain, so 16 races were run. The season ran from 28 May (Hockenheim) to 29 Oct (Hockenheim).
Bernd Schneider won the title in the D2 AMG-Mercedes CLK with 221 points, ahead of Manuel Reuter (Opel, 162) and Klaus Ludwig (Mercedes, 122). It was Schneider's second DTM/ITC-era title after 1995.

## Regulations
- **Concept:** a silhouette formula with front engines and rear-wheel drive.
  - Eligible base models were 2-door, 4-seat coupés with at least 10,000 units built in 12 months [evo].
  - Each car had a tubular steel spaceframe with a carbon safety cell. Bodywork was mostly carbon; only the roof and door skins came from the road car [evo].
- **Dimensions (evo):** minimum wheelbase 2500 mm; length 4300–4700 mm; minimum height 1350 mm (as printed by evo; actual cars were about 1250 mm, see car table); maximum width 1850 mm.
- **Engine:**
  - 4.0 L naturally aspirated 90° V8, DOHC, 4 valves per cylinder.
  - Two 28 mm air restrictors, rev limit 8000 rpm, minimum engine weight 165 kg [evo; en.wiki].
  - Engines were sealed for the season. Changing one mid-season carried heavy penalties [evo].
  - Bosch supplied the control ECU, injectors, sensors and similar parts [en.wiki].
- **Minimum weight:** 1080 kg including driver [evo]. Other secondary sources quote 1050 kg or "1000 kg without driver" (unverified).
- **Aero:** the first 900 mm of the front end was free. Underbody, rear wing and diffuser were standardised [evo].
- **Drivetrain:** X-Trac 6-speed sequential transaxle with fixed ratios (only the final drive was free) and a mechanical LSD [evo, for the CLK].
- **Electronics:** ABS and traction control were banned [totalmotorsport 2002 regs; assumed the same in 2000].
- **Brakes:** AP Racing carbon/carbon, 3 sets per season [evo].
- **Tyres:**
  - Dunlop SP Sport Maxx was the single control tyre.
  - Sizes: front 240/640-R18 on 10-inch rims, rear 280/650-R18 on 12-inch rims.
  - **5 sets per race weekend** [evo].
- **Success ballast / weight penalties:** none documented in the sources checked (see Unverified).
- **Pit stops / refuelling:** no mandatory stops documented for 2000.

## Weekend format and points
- **Qualifying** set the grid for race 1. The race 2 grid rule is not documented.
- **Two races** per event: about 100 km or 40 minutes each, with a 15-minute gap between them (en.wiki, evo). Both races paid equal points.
- **Starts:** rolling starts. Standing starts only came in 2002.
- **Points:** 20-15-12-10-8-6-4-3-2-1 to the top 10 in each race. All results counted. No points for pole or fastest lap [de/en.wiki].
- **Teams classification:** each 2-car pair counts as one team. Teams running 3 or more cars were split into 2-car entries [speedsport-magazine].
- **Manufacturers' championship:** none in 2000.

## Teams and people
- **HWA:** ran the Mercedes works effort as two squads: D2 (#1/#2) and Warsteiner (#5/#6).
- **Mercedes customer teams:** Persson Motorsport (Original-Teile) and Team Rosberg (24h Service).
- **Rosberg numbers:** Rosberg changed from #14/#15 to #24/#42 when sponsor Service 24h arrived. de.wiki says from rd 2; en.wiki says from rd 3.
- **Opel:** all four Opel teams came from the STW: Holzer (2 pairs), Phoenix, Irmscher and Euroteam.
- **Abt Sportsline:** team boss Hans-Jürgen Abt (de.wiki). Abt ran two pairs; Audi did not enter as a manufacturer.
- **Other team principals:** not documented in the sources checked. General knowledge (unverified): HWA = Hans-Werner Aufrecht, Phoenix = Ernst Moser, Rosberg = Keke Rosberg, Persson = Ingmar Persson.

## Storylines
- **The comeback.** The DTM returned after its collapse at the end of 1996 and the end of the STW. Mercedes and Opel took part as works teams. Audi was represented only by Abt's semi-private TT-R.
- **Schneider's dominance.** Schneider won 6 races. He beat Reuter, who took 4 wins for Phoenix Opel.
- **Audi's struggle.** The TT-R was well off the pace. Aïello's 14 points were the best Audi result. Aïello and Abt missed Oschersleben (rd 2), where Roland Asch and James Thompson drove.
- **Lausitz cancelled.** The Lausitz event (3 Sep) was abandoned in torrential rain after qualifying.
- **Euroteam:** en.wiki links Euroteam to ByKolles (Kolles family), not verified.
