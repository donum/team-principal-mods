# 2015 DTM: research dossier

## Overview
- 9 weekends with 2 races each, so 18 races. The season ran from 2 May (Hockenheim) to 18 October (Hockenheim).
- 3 makes with 8 cars each, 24 cars in total: Audi RS 5 DTM, BMW M4 DTM and Mercedes-AMG C63 DTM. The Mercedes was the renamed "DTM AMG Mercedes C-Coupé".
- Champions:
  - Drivers: **Pascal Wehrlein** (HWA, Mercedes) with 169 points
  - Teams: gooix/Original-Teile Mercedes-AMG (HWA)
  - Manufacturers: BMW
- A very open season: 13 different drivers won the 18 races. Only the top three in the championship won more than one race (de.wiki).

## Regulations
- **Format:** two races per weekend, back for the first time since 2002, now on Saturday and Sunday. Both races were time-limited:
  - Saturday: 40 min. No mandatory stop.
  - Sunday: 60 min. One mandatory pit stop.
  - Search results describe both races as "+1 lap".
  - Equal points for both races.
- **Qualifying:** a separate 20-minute single session before each race. This replaced the knock-out format.
- **Points:** 25-18-15-12-10-8-6-4-2-1 for the top 10. No qualifying points.
- **Tyres:** Hankook, single supplier.
  - The softer "option" tyre was dropped for 2015 (en.wiki).
  - 4 sets of slicks per car for both qualifying sessions and both races. Sunday required 2 sets, so one set had to be used twice.
- **DRS:** usable up to 3 times per lap, previously once. Allowed when within 1.0 s of the car ahead, previously 2.0 s. There were no fixed zones; drivers chose where to use it.
- **Weight:** 1,120 kg minimum, raised for all cars.
  - **Performance weights** were introduced from round 3 (Norisring). They were set per driver from the previous race's results.
  - The range was 1,105–1,140 kg (en.wiki).
- **Engines:** one engine per driver for the whole season. Each manufacturer had 1 spare engine for all its drivers, previously 2.
  - BMW exceeded its allocation once. Its best-placed driver was then not counted for the manufacturers' score at that race (de.wiki).
- **Curfew:** 11 hours with no work overnight from Saturday to Sunday. Each driver had one "joker" per season to break it.
- **Car numbers:** permanent driver numbers from 2 to 99 (100). Number 1 was reserved for the champion.

## Cars and engines
- All three cars used naturally aspirated 4.0 L V8 engines with a 90° V-angle, about 460 hp. BMW and Mercedes quoted 7,500 rpm.
  - Audi: Audi V8
  - BMW: P66
  - Mercedes: built by HWA
- Gearbox: Hewland, paddle-shift. Hankook tyres. Fuel: Aral Ultimate 102 RON.
- BMW M4 DTM dimensions: 4,775–5,010 mm long, 1,950 mm wide, 1,200 mm high, 2,750 mm wheelbase (en.wiki).

## Storylines
- Mercedes added an eighth car and brought ART Grand Prix in. HWA dropped from 5 cars to 4.
- Rookies:
  - Lucas Auer and Tom Blomqvist, from F3
  - Maximilian Götz, the 2014 Blancpain Sprint champion
- Leaving: Joey Hand and Vitaly Petrov.
- **Red Bull Ring race 2:** Timo Scheider pushed Robert Wickens and Pascal Wehrlein off.
  - Audi motorsport chief Ullrich was linked to the order over the radio.
  - Audi was given **0 manufacturer points** for that race.
  - Scheider was banned for Moscow and replaced by Antonio Giovinazzi.
- Wehrlein moved to F1 (Manor) for 2016.
- Hungaroring dropped after one year.
